About Our Service

Do you provide the moving truck?+

No. We are a moving labor company — we provide the crew, not the vehicle. You arrange your own truck rental (U-Haul, Penske, Budget, etc.), trailer, PODS, or moving container. We handle the loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly around whatever transportation you've arranged.

What exactly is "moving labor"?+

Moving labor refers to the physical crew work that happens around transportation — loading the truck, unloading at the destination, packing boxes, unpacking, and assembling or disassembling furniture. It's the part of moving that requires trained hands and heavy lifting. A moving labor company provides those hands; you provide the vehicle.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Yes. Rest Assured Moving LLC is a licensed business and carries appropriate liability insurance. If you need specific documentation for a job, contact us and we'll provide it.

What areas do you serve?+

We serve Salem, Keizer, Woodburn, Albany, McMinnville, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, and the greater Portland Metro area. If your move starts or ends in this region, we can typically help — contact us to confirm your specific location.

On Moving Day

What should I have ready when the crew arrives?+

For the smoothest start: (1) your truck, trailer, or container parked and accessible, (2) hallways and doorways cleared of rugs, plants, and clutter, (3) boxes sealed and labeled by room if you packed them yourself, (4) any fragile or special-handling items pointed out before loading begins, and (5) someone present to answer questions and confirm furniture placement.

What if the job takes longer than the estimate?+

The estimate is our best projection based on the information you provide. If the job runs longer, you pay for the actual hours worked at the quoted rate — never more than the agreed rate per hour. If we anticipate the job will significantly exceed the estimate, we'll tell you before continuing, not after.

Do the movers take breaks?+

For jobs over 4 hours, we build in short rest breaks — typically 10–15 minutes. We do not charge for these breaks. They're built into how we schedule longer jobs, not tacked onto your total hours.

What if something gets damaged during my move?+

Contact us directly, as soon as possible, with a description of the damage and photos if possible. We take damage reports seriously and handle them directly — no runaround. We are licensed and insured. Our goal is to prevent damage in the first place through proper wrapping and handling technique; if it happens, we want to make it right.

Should I tip the crew?+

Tips are not expected but are always appreciated for a job well done. If the crew exceeded your expectations, a tip is a meaningful way to recognize that. It goes directly to the movers who did the work.

Services

Can I book just loading, or just unloading — not both?+

Yes. Many customers book only one end of their move — loading at the origin, unloading at the destination, or both. You can also book packing, unpacking, or furniture assembly as completely standalone services.

Do you handle oversized or specialty items like pianos or gun safes?+

Some oversized items can be accommodated — contact us before booking to discuss the specific item, its weight, and the access at both ends. Pianos, gun safes, and pool tables often require specialty equipment or additional crew. We'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit for your specific piece.

Do you disassemble and reassemble furniture?+

Yes — both. We can disassemble before the move so furniture fits safely through doorways and into your truck, then reassemble at the destination. Hardware is bagged and labeled by component so nothing gets lost or mixed up.

Can you do furniture assembly even if I'm not doing a full move?+

Absolutely. Furniture assembly is one of our most popular standalone services. New flat-pack furniture (IKEA, Wayfair, etc.), reassembly after storage, or setting up a home office — no move required to book this service.

What happens to packing debris (boxes, paper, wrap) after unpacking?+

We break down all flattened boxes and consolidate packing paper and bubble wrap. We'll stack everything neatly for you to arrange disposal, recycling, or donation — or let us know upfront if you'd like us to take it with us and we'll discuss that option.

What We Don't Provide

Do you drive the truck or provide transportation?+

No. We are a moving labor company. We do not drive trucks, own vehicles for hire, or provide transportation between locations. You arrange your own rental truck, trailer, PODS, or container — we provide the crew that loads, unloads, packs, and assembles around it.

Can you move me long-distance to another state?+

We can provide loading labor in our service area before a long-distance move, and unloading labor at the destination if it falls within our area. However, we don't provide transportation or cross-state hauling. If you're arranging your own truck or container for a long-distance move, we can handle the labor on the local end.

Do you provide storage?+

We don't operate storage facilities. If you're moving items into a storage unit and need loading help, we can assist with that labor. For the storage unit itself, we're happy to recommend local options in the Salem area.
