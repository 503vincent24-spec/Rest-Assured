# SEO Content — Rest Assured Moving LLC
Target market: Salem, Oregon moving labor (loading/unloading/packing/unpacking/furniture assembly).
Explicitly NOT targeting transportation/hauling keywords — this business does not advertise
or provide trucking/freight services, per brand guidelines.

================================================================================
PRIMARY KEYWORDS (from original brief)
================================================================================
1. moving labor Salem Oregon
2. loading and unloading services
3. packing and unpacking services
4. furniture assembly Salem

================================================================================
SUPPORTING / LONG-TAIL KEYWORDS (recommended additions)
================================================================================
- moving help Salem OR
- moving crew for rental truck
- POD loading and unloading
- pack and unpack moving boxes
- furniture disassembly and reassembly
- local movers Salem Oregon (labor only — use carefully, see note below)
- moving labor near me
- hourly moving help Salem
- furniture assembly service near me
- help loading U-Haul Salem

NOTE on "movers": Many searchers type "movers Salem Oregon" expecting full-service
moving companies that include transportation. Since this business is labor-only, pages
targeting that term should clarify the labor-only model in the first 1–2 sentences of
on-page copy (already done on index.html, services.html, and about.html) so visitors
and search engines don't form an incorrect impression of the service.
