# Nextdoor Business Profile Guide — Rest Assured Moving LLC

Nextdoor is the hyperlocal social network where homeowners and renters get
neighbor-to-neighbor recommendations. For a local moving labor business, a
single Nextdoor recommendation from a satisfied customer can generate 3–5
bookings from neighbors who saw it — at zero cost.

---

## 1. Why Nextdoor Matters

Moving is a neighborhood event. When someone posts "does anyone know a good
moving crew?" on Nextdoor, neighbors respond with real, trusted recommendations.
The Salem/Keizer/Portland Metro audience skews 35–65 homeowners — exactly
who hires moving labor.

## 4. How Recommendations Work

**Organic (free, most valuable):** Neighbors respond to "who do you know?"
posts with your name. You cannot buy these. They're earned by doing
excellent work and asking satisfied customers to mention you on Nextdoor.

**Business recommendations:** Neighbors can proactively recommend your
business page without a prompt. These appear on your profile and in feeds.

**Paid placement:** Local Deals or Neighborhood Sponsorship ads.
See ADVERTISING_STRATEGY.md for details.

After every completed job in your service area:

> "If you're on Nextdoor and happy with how today went, a quick mention

## 6. Posting Strategy

**Frequency:** Once per week maximum — Nextdoor penalizes over-posting.
Once every 10–14 days is safer for long-term standing.

**Post formats that work:**

*Local service availability:*
```
Rest Assured Moving LLC — moving labor in [NEIGHBORHOOD]
Loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly.
Flat hourly rate, no fuel fees, free quotes.
(971) 302-0120 | [WEBSITE URL]
```

*Helpful tip (performs better than pure ads):*
```
Moving tip from the Rest Assured crew: measure your doorways AND your
furniture before moving day. A couch that fits through one door won't
always fit through another — 5 minutes now saves a lot of frustration.
We're Salem's labor-only moving crew. [WEBSITE URL]
```

*Seasonal:*
```
Summer moves are booking up. Rest Assured Moving LLC has availability
for loading, unloading, and assembly across Salem and surrounding area.
Free quotes — (971) 302-0120.
```

## 8. Responding to Recommendation Requests

Business accounts generally cannot reply to organic "who do you know?" posts
(Nextdoor restricts this to prevent spam). What you CAN do:

- Keep your profile polished so when you ARE recommended, the page they
  find looks professional
- Encourage customers to watch for those posts in their own neighborhoods
  and mention you by name on their personal accounts

A neighbor recommendation on someone's personal account is the highest-value
marketing touchpoint on the platform — it cannot be purchased.

---

## 9. Nextdoor vs. Other Platforms

| Goal | Best channel |
|---|---|
| High-intent search traffic | Google (LSA + Search) |
| Neighborhood trust/recommendations | Nextdoor |
| Broad awareness + retargeting | Facebook/Instagram |
| Business info + reviews | Google Business Profile |
| Frequent posting + community | Facebook page |

Nextdoor is additive — not a replacement for other channels. Its unique
value is hyperlocal, neighbor-endorsed trust at minimal cost.
