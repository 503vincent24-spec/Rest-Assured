does not constitute legal advice. Have this document reviewed by a licensed
Oregon attorney before using it with customers. Laws governing moving
contracts, liability, and consumer protection vary and change. Do not rely
on this template as legal protection without professional review.

---

## How to Use This Template

**For phone/email bookings:** Email the completed agreement to the customer
before the job. Ask them to reply "I agree" or sign digitally (DocuSign,
SignNow, or a PDF signature). Keep a copy for your records.

**For in-person:** Print the completed form and have the customer sign at the
start of the job, before any work begins.

**At minimum:** Every customer should receive a written quote (email is fine)
stating the rate, services, and date. The full agreement below adds additional
protection for both parties.

### Labor-Only Service — Customer Responsibilities

Rest Assured Moving LLC is a **moving labor company only**. This agreement
covers labor services exclusively. The customer is responsible for:

1. Providing, reserving, and having ready on the date of service their own
   truck, trailer, PODS, moving container, or other transportation vessel
2. Ensuring the truck or container is properly parked and accessible at the
   pickup address at the agreed start time
3. Providing sufficient fuel, keys, and any access codes needed for the
   pickup and delivery locations

Rest Assured Moving LLC does not own, operate, or provide any vehicle,
truck, trailer, or transportation service. This agreement does not include
any freight, hauling, or transportation between locations.

### Damage Policy

Rest Assured Moving LLC will handle all items with reasonable professional
care. In the event of damage to customer property caused by Rest Assured
Moving LLC personnel during the performance of services:

1. The customer must report any damage to the crew lead or owner before the
   crew departs the job site.
2. Damage reported after the crew departs may not be accepted as the
   responsibility of Rest Assured Moving LLC.
3. Rest Assured Moving LLC carries general liability insurance. Claims will
   be evaluated based on the circumstances and handled directly between the
   owner and the customer.
4. Rest Assured Moving LLC is not responsible for damage to items that were:
   - Already damaged prior to the service
   - Improperly packed by the customer prior to the crew's arrival
   - Specifically identified by the customer as requiring special handling
     that was not communicated before the job began
   - Furniture, items, or materials that are inherently fragile, unstable,
     or unable to withstand normal moving procedures

**Pre-existing conditions:** The customer is encouraged to identify any
items of particular value, fragility, or special concern before work begins.

beyond its reasonable control, including but not limited to: traffic,
weather, customer-caused delays (truck not ready, access issues, items not
packed), or building/elevator restrictions.
