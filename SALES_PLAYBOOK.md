# Sales Playbook — Rest Assured Moving LLC

The website, listings, and ads bring in leads. This playbook turns leads
into **booked, bigger jobs** and repeat business. Keep it next to the
phone.

---

## 1. The Revenue Math

```
Revenue = Leads × Booking rate × Average job size × (1 + repeat/referral)
```

| Lever | Typical starting point | Target | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leads per week | 5–10 | 20+ | `GET_CUSTOMERS_NOW.md`, `ONLINE_LISTINGS_LAUNCH_KIT.md` |
| Booking rate | 25–35% | **50%+** | Answer in 15 minutes, quote on the first call (§2) |
| Average job | ~$250 (2 movers × ~2.5 hrs) | **$400+** | Upsells in §3 |
| Repeat + referral | — | 1 in 4 jobs | §5 |

At 20 leads, 50% booked, and $400 a job, that's **about $4,000 a week**.
Raising the booking rate and the average job size costs nothing extra in
advertising.

## 2. Quote and Book on the First Call

Respond to every call, text, form, and app lead **within 15 minutes**
(5 minutes on Angi and Thumbtack). The first mover who responds usually
wins the job.

**The 5 questions (have the customer's answers before giving a price):**
1. What's the move date, and is it flexible?
2. How big is the home, and are there stairs, an elevator, or a long walk
   to parking?
3. Which truck or container are you using, and what size?
4. Which cities are you moving from and to? Do you need help at one end
   or both?
5. Anything that needs packing, taking apart, or putting back together?

**Give the price on the call.** Use the table on the homepage or
`estimate.html`:
> "For a 2-bedroom on the second floor, loading and unloading, I'd send
> 3 movers at $130 an hour. That usually runs 4½ to 6½ hours, so plan on
> roughly $585 to $845. No fuel or mileage fees, and blankets and wrap are
> included."

**Ask for the booking:**
> "I've got a crew open on Saturday morning at 8. Want me to put you down
> for it?"

**If they hesitate:**

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. Weekends near the end of the month fill up fast, so I can hold Saturday 8am until tomorrow at noon. Want me to do that?" |
| "Someone else is cheaper" | "Check whether their price includes blankets and wrap, and whether they charge travel or fuel. Ours doesn't. A crew that loads the truck right saves you a second trip, too." |
| "Do you have a truck?" | "We're labor-only, which is why we cost less than full-service movers. Book a U-Haul, Penske, or PODS, and I can tell you what size you need." |
| "Can you do it cheaper?" | "I can't lower the hourly rate, but I can lower your total. If you have boxes packed and beds stripped before we arrive, the job goes faster." |

**Confirm in writing right away**: date, arrival window, crew size, rate,
addresses, and what's included. Use template 2 in `EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md`.
Send a reminder text the day before.

## 3. Make Each Job Bigger (and better for the customer)

Offer each of these when it fits. Each one solves a real problem, so it
doesn't feel like a pushy upsell.

| Offer | When | Script | Typical added revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unload at the new place too** | They booked loading only | "Who's unloading at the other end? We can meet you there, and you'll know it's done the same way." | +$190 to $500 |
| **Third mover** | 2-bedroom or larger, stairs, or a tight truck return time | "A third mover usually finishes faster, so it costs about the same and you get your day back." | Shorter job, bigger crew, happier customer |
| **Packing the day before** | They "still have a lot to pack" | "Most people underestimate packing by half. We can come the day before and pack the kitchen and fragile items properly." | +$190 to $400 |
| **Furniture take-down and rebuild** | Bed frames, cribs, desks, sectionals | "We'll take the beds apart for the move and have them built again before you need to sleep." | +30 to 60 min |
| **Unpacking / room setup** | Families, seniors, busy professionals | "We can unpack the kitchen and set up the bedrooms so you're living in the place tonight, not next month." | +$190 to $380 |
| **Storage unit** | Staging or downsizing | "Want us to load the storage unit while we're at it? It's on the way." | +1 to 2 hrs |

## 4. Fill the Schedule and Earn More Per Hour

- **Busiest times:** the last 3 days and first 3 days of each month,
  Saturdays, and May–September. Push leads to book these slots early.
  Once your calendar fills regularly, consider a weekend or
  end-of-month rate a little above your base rate. That's your call.
- **Slow times:** mid-week, mid-month, and the November–February winter
  months. Fill them with **furniture assembly** (TaskRabbit, Craigslist
  Ad 4), storage moves, and partner referrals. Mention flexibility
  ("Tuesday is wide open if your date can move") to fill gaps.
- **Stack jobs geographically.** Book Portland Metro jobs on the same
  day so you aren't driving back and forth.
- **Keep the 2-hour minimum.** It protects small jobs from losing money.

## 5. Repeat Customers and Referrals

1. **Before leaving:** walk through the home with the customer, hand over
   two business cards ("one for you, one for whoever moves next"), and
   ask for a **Google** review (`REVIEW_MANAGEMENT_SYSTEM.md`). Never ask
   for Yelp reviews.
2. **Day 1:** thank-you text with the Google review link.
3. **Day 30:** "Hope you're settled in! If you need anything rearranged
   or more furniture built, we're a text away."
4. **Referral partners:** U-Haul dealers, storage facilities, apartment
   managers, and real estate agents (`REFERRAL_PARTNER_TOOLKIT.md`).
   Visit each once a month with cards. One busy apartment complex can
   refer several jobs a month.
5. **Real reviews on the website:** once you have 3+ genuine reviews,
   add them to the homepage (see the comment above the promise section in
   `index.html`). Use first name and last initial, and get the customer's
   permission.

## 6. Following Up on Quotes That Didn't Book

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| Same day | "Thanks for talking with me, [Name]! I'm holding [date/time] for you until [deadline]. Just reply YES to lock it in." |
| Day 2 | "Hi [Name], just checking in on your move on [date]. Any questions I can answer?" |
| 3 days before their move date | "If your plans changed or you still need hands on [date], we have a crew available." |

Stop after three messages. Mark the lead as lost and note why in the
log: price, date, they found someone else, or no answer.
