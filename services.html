Itemized & transparent

Every job, broken down to the line item.

No vague "moving help" packages. Here's exactly what's covered under loading and unloading services, packing and unpacking services, and furniture assembly in Salem — so you know precisely what you're booking.

01

Loading & Unloading

Our crews load and unload your rental truck, moving container, or POD with an eye for protecting your belongings and using every inch of space wisely — so you're not paying for a second trip.

  • Furniture wrapped in moving blankets & shrink wrap
  • Strategic load plan to maximize truck/container space
  • Careful handling of mirrors, glass, and electronics
  • Unloading and placement at the new address, room by room
  • Walkthrough at the end to confirm nothing was missed
02

Packing & Unpacking

Full or partial packing services, including the fragile items people dread packing themselves — plus unpacking on the other end so your new place feels like home faster.

  • Room-by-room or full-house packing
  • Specialty packing for dishware, glassware, and artwork
  • Clear labeling by room and contents
  • Unpacking boxes and placing items in cabinets, closets, shelves
  • Flattened boxes and packing debris removed after unpacking
03

Furniture Assembly & Disassembly

Furniture assembly in Salem for everything from bed frames to bookshelves — taken apart before the move so it fits safely in the truck, then rebuilt correctly once you've arrived.

  • Bed frames, headboards, and bunk beds
  • Dining tables, desks, and bookshelves
  • Sectionals and modular furniture
  • Hardware bagged, labeled, and kept with the matching piece
  • Reassembly at your new home, placed where you want it
Good to know

We're labor — not the truck.

Rest Assured Moving LLC is a moving labor company. We don't own or operate moving trucks, and we don't provide transportation or freight-hauling services between locations.

Most of our customers have already rented a truck, reserved a POD or moving container, or hired a trailer — and just need an experienced crew to handle the loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly around it. If that's your situation, we're built for exactly that.

Typical jobs we're booked for

  • Loading a U-Haul, Penske, or Budget rental truck
  • Loading or unloading a PODS / moving container
  • Help on both ends of a move (loading day + unloading day)
  • Packing before movers arrive, or unpacking after they leave
  • Furniture assembly only — no full move needed
