Transparent & itemized

Every service, broken down to the line.

No vague "moving help" packages. Here's exactly what's included in each service — so you know precisely what you're booking and what it covers.

Jump to: Loading Unloading Packing Unpacking Assembly Disassembly
01

Loading Services

We load your rental truck, trailer, POD, or moving container with a plan — not just "put stuff in until it's full." Proper loading protects your belongings in transit and maximizes every cubic foot so you don't pay for a second trip.

Best for: rental truck loading, PODS and moving container loading, loading one end of a cross-state move before you drive.

  • Pre-load walkthrough to plan order and protect fragile items
  • All furniture wrapped in moving blankets and/or shrink wrap
  • Strategic load plan — heaviest items low, fragile items protected
  • Navigation of stairs, elevators, and narrow doorways
  • Maximum use of truck/container cubic footage
  • Final walkthrough to confirm nothing was left behind
02

Unloading Services

We unload at your new address and place everything where you actually want it — not just wherever there's floor space. Every box goes to its labeled room. Every piece of furniture gets positioned before we leave.

Best for: unloading at a new home or apartment, unloading a PODS delivery, having help when rental truck arrives ahead of you.

  • Organized unload — boxes to labeled rooms, furniture placed
  • Direction taken from you on furniture placement
  • Careful handling through doorways and up/down stairs
  • Moving blankets and wrapping materials removed and returned
  • Items placed so your home is immediately functional
  • Final walkthrough confirming all items accounted for
03

Packing Services

Improper packing is the leading cause of move-related damage. Our crew uses the right materials and the right techniques — edge-stacked plates, crumpled-paper cushioning, individually wrapped glassware — so nothing arrives broken.

Best for: full-home pack-outs, partial packing (just the kitchen, just the fragile items), specialty packing for art and electronics.

  • Room-by-room or full-house packing available
  • Specialty packing for dishware, glassware, and stemware
  • Fragile-item wrapping for mirrors, art, and electronics
  • Correct box weight limits respected to prevent breakage
  • Clear labeling by room and contents on every box
  • "Fragile" and orientation markings applied where needed
04

Unpacking Services

The part everyone abandons in the garage for three weeks. We unpack boxes and place items where they belong — cabinets, closets, shelves, drawers — so your new home functions from day one, not day twenty-two.

Best for: settling in after a move, unpacking after a PODS delivery, unpacking while you handle other post-move logistics.

  • Box unpacking and item placement per your direction
  • Kitchen, bathroom, and bedroom unpacking prioritized if requested
  • Items placed in cabinets, closets, and on shelves
  • All flattened boxes broken down and stacked for disposal
  • Packing paper and bubble wrap consolidated and removed
  • Home left clean and functional, not full of empty boxes
05

Furniture Assembly

Bed frames, bookshelves, dining tables, sectionals, office desks — assembled correctly, on the first attempt. Hardware bagged and labeled by component from the start, so nothing gets lost and nothing gets over-tightened.

Best for: assembly after a move, new furniture from IKEA or other flat-pack retailers, reassembly after long-term storage. Available as a standalone service — no full move required.

  • Bed frames, headboards, footboards, and bunk beds
  • Dining tables, desks, and office furniture
  • Bookshelves, modular shelving, and entertainment centers
  • Sectionals and modular sofa components
  • Hardware sorted, bagged by component — nothing left over
  • Pieces confirmed level and structurally sound before we leave
06

Furniture Disassembly

Most large furniture must come apart to fit safely through doorways, stairwells, and into a truck or container. We disassemble before the move — hardware bagged and labeled per piece — so reassembly at the other end is straightforward and nothing gets stripped or lost.

Best for: disassembly before a loading day, large furniture that won't fit assembled through new-home doorways, pieces that need to lay flat in a truck.

  • Bed frames, headboards, and platform beds
  • Large dining tables and extension tables
  • Sectionals and modular furniture
  • Bookshelves, wardrobe units, and large desks
  • All hardware bagged and labeled by piece — not one big bag
  • Photos taken before disassembly on complex pieces, if helpful

Common questions

Quick answers about our services

Can I book just loading, or just unloading?+

Yes — many customers book us for only one end of their move. Loading only, unloading only, or both. We also handle packing and furniture assembly as completely standalone services with no move required.

Do you supply packing materials?+

We supply moving blankets and shrink wrap for furniture protection — those are included in the hourly rate. Boxes, packing paper, and tape can be provided at an additional cost, or you can supply your own and we'll pack with them.

How long does a typical job take?+

A studio or 1-bedroom load typically takes 2–3 hours with a crew of two. A 3-bedroom home with furniture assembly can take 5–8 hours with a crew of three or four. We'll give you an estimated time range when we quote your specific job.

What if I only need furniture assembled — no move?+

That's a common booking. Furniture assembly is available as a standalone service — whether it's new flat-pack furniture, reassembly after a move you already handled, or pieces that came out of storage.
