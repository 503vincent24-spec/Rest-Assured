## 2a. Turning Messages into Booked Jobs

Most Instagram customers will **message** you instead of filling out the
form. How fast you reply decides whether you get the job.

**Turn on automatic replies.** In Meta Business Suite → Inbox →
Automations (covers Instagram and Facebook together):

- **Instant reply** (sent the moment someone messages):
  ```
  Thanks for reaching out to Rest Assured Moving! 👋 For a fast quote,
  send: 1) your move date, 2) home size + any stairs, 3) the truck or
  container you're using, 4) pickup & drop-off cities. We reply within
  the hour, Mon–Sat 7am–7pm. Prefer to talk? Call/text (971) 302-0120.
  ```
- **Frequently asked questions** (tap-to-ask buttons in the chat):
  - *How much does it cost?* → "$95/hr for 2 movers, $130/hr for 3, 2-hour minimum. Blankets & wrap included, no fuel or mileage fees. Most 1-bedroom loads run $190–$238."
  - *Do you bring a truck?* → "We're labor-only, which keeps us cheaper than full-service movers. Book a U-Haul, Penske, or PODS, and we'll load and unload it."
  - *What areas do you cover?* → "Salem, Keizer, Woodburn, Albany, McMinnville, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard & Portland Metro."
  - *How do I book?* → "Send your move date and home size here, or text (971) 302-0120. We'll confirm the crew and rate in writing."
- **Away message** (outside 7am–7pm): "We're off the clock but will reply first thing in the morning. Send your move date and home size and we'll have a quote ready."

**Then reply personally within 15 minutes** during business hours. Use
the 5 questions and the "ask for the booking" line in
`SALES_PLAYBOOK.md` §2. Move to a phone call or text as soon as you can,
because jobs get booked faster that way.

**End every post** with one clear call to action: *"Text (971) 302-0120"*,
*"DM us your move date"*, or *"Free quote: link in bio"*.

## 2c. Measuring What Instagram Actually Brings In

Each month, check:
- **Lead emails** with `utm_source=instagram` (from the bio link and
  story links)
- **"Heard about us: Instagram"** on the quote form (now in the menu)
- **DMs and calls** that say they found you on Instagram (ask every caller
  "How did you find us?")
- **Profile → Professional dashboard → Insights**: profile visits,
  link taps, and Call/Text button taps

If Instagram produces fewer than 1–2 booked jobs a month after 90 days,
cut back to reposting Facebook content once a week and put the time into
Google reviews and the Moving Help marketplace instead.

## 3. Content Strategy: What Performs on Instagram

Instagram's algorithm rewards: **Reels (short video) > Carousels > single images > text-only**.

| Format | Organic reach | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Reels (15–60 sec) | Highest | New audience reach; tips; time-lapses |
| Carousels (2–10 slides) | High | Step-by-step guides; before/after; service breakdowns |
| Single image | Medium | Testimonials; quotes; team photos |
| Stories | Low (reach) but high engagement for existing followers | Polls, Q&As, behind-the-scenes |

## 4. Reels Content Ideas (phone-filmed, 15–30 seconds each)

These can be filmed on any recent smartphone. Authenticity outperforms polish.

| Reel concept | How to film | Caption angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Time-lapse load** | Mount phone on truck interior, film from empty to full | "This took us 2 hours. Here it is in 30 seconds." |
| **Assembly reveal** | Film flat-pack pile → finished assembled furniture in same frame | "IKEA bed frame. 45 minutes. No leftover screws." |
| **Wrapping technique** | Film crew wrapping a dresser, 15 seconds | "This is why your furniture doesn't get scratched." |
| **Edge-stacked plates** | Film the correct vs. incorrect plate packing | "Stop stacking plates flat. Here's why." |
| **The walkthrough** | Film a quick pre-job walkthrough (with customer permission) | "We do this before touching anything." |
| **Crew intro** | 5-second each crew member introduction | "This is the team that shows up for your move." |
| **Moving day tip** | Face camera, give one specific tip in 20 seconds | "The one thing to do the night before moving day…" |
| **Hardware system** | Film hardware bags being labeled per component | "One bag per bolt group. Nothing gets lost." |

**Reel caption structure:**
```
Hook (first line — visible before "more"): The specific outcome or surprising fact
Body: 2–3 sentences of context or instruction
CTA: "Free quote in bio" or "DM us [city] to get started"
Hashtags: 5–8 relevant hashtags
```

## 8. Story Content Cadence

Post 3–5 Stories per week. Stories disappear after 24 hours but build daily habits for your followers.

| Story type | Frequency | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Behind the scenes | 2–3x/week | Morning crew prep, equipment load, driving to a job |
| Tip or fact | 1–2x/week | Single moving tip with text overlay |
| Poll or question | 1x/week | "Dreading the packing or the lifting more?" |
| Promotional | 1x/week max | Quote CTA, seasonal availability reminder |
| Repost from feed | As needed | Share new Reels or carousels to Stories for extended reach |

## 10. Cross-Platform Content Adaptation

Facebook posts → Instagram: Shorten the caption significantly (Instagram rewards brevity), add hashtags (Facebook doesn't benefit from hashtags), and ensure any linked content is referenced as "link in bio" not as a clickable URL.

Instagram Reels → Facebook: Download finished Reels and repost natively to Facebook Reels for additional reach. Do not cross-post by linking — native uploads always outperform shared links.

Blog posts → Instagram Carousel: Take the key steps from each blog post (e.g., the 10 moving day tips) and turn each into a slide. End with a "Save this" or "Free quote: link in bio" call to action.

## 11. Instagram vs. Facebook: Key Differences

| Element | Instagram | Facebook |
|---|---|---|
| Best audience age | 25–45 | 30–60 |
| Link placement | Bio only (no clickable links in captions) | Links work in posts |
| Video priority | Reels dominant | Reels + video, but text also works |
| Hashtag value | Yes — use 5–8 | Minimal value |
| Local search | Less direct | Google + Nextdoor still stronger locally |
| Profile visit → booking path | Bio link → website | Post CTA → direct message or link |
