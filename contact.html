Frequently asked

Do you provide the moving truck?

No. We're a moving labor company — we handle loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly. You arrange your own truck rental, trailer, or moving container.

Can I book you for just one end of the move?

Yes. Many customers book us only to load on one end or only to unload on the other. You can also book both.

How is pricing structured?

We quote a flat hourly rate based on crew size and job scope, confirmed before the work begins — no fuel, mileage, or surprise fees.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 1–2 weeks ahead, especially around the 1st and last weekend of the month. We'll always try to accommodate shorter notice when our schedule allows.

Do you disassemble and reassemble furniture?

Yes — bed frames, tables, shelving, and sectionals are common requests. Hardware is bagged and labeled so reassembly at your new place is quick and correct.
