# Job Site Checklist — Rest Assured Moving LLC
Printed or phone-based checklist for crew use on every job.
Nothing gets skipped because it "seemed obvious."

---

## Before Leaving for the Job

- [ ] Moving blankets loaded (minimum [#] per crew member)
- [ ] Shrink wrap rolls loaded (minimum [#])
- [ ] Dollies and hand trucks loaded (at least one per crew member)
- [ ] Floor runners / carpet protectors loaded (if available)
- [ ] Basic tool kit packed: Allen keys, screwdrivers, rubber mallet
- [ ] Hardware bags and labels (for furniture disassembly/assembly jobs)
- [ ] Work gloves for every crew member
- [ ] Phone charged and job address confirmed in maps
- [ ] Customer phone number saved in contacts
- [ ] Job details reviewed: services booked, special items noted, crew size confirmed

---

## On Arrival

- [ ] Call/text customer: "We're [X] minutes away"
- [ ] Park in a position that gives clear access to the entrance — not in a way that blocks the truck spot
- [ ] Do not begin unloading equipment until the customer is present or has granted access
- [ ] Introduce yourself and the crew by name
- [ ] Present a professional appearance — clean clothes, no offensive graphics

---

## Pre-Job Walkthrough (Do this before touching anything)

Walk every room with the customer and confirm:

**What moves today:**
- [ ] Confirmed which rooms/items are in scope
- [ ] Confirmed which items do NOT move (staging furniture, items being donated, etc.)
- [ ] Any items left in place clearly identified (tape marking is helpful for large rooms)

**Special items:**
- [ ] Fragile items (mirrors, glass-top tables, artwork) identified and noted
- [ ] Piano, safe, or oversized items noted — special handling confirmed
- [ ] Electronics confirmed — cables photographed if customer hasn't already
- [ ] Items requiring disassembly before loading identified

**Access and logistics:**
- [ ] Elevator reserved/confirmed if high-rise
- [ ] Stairwells walked — note any tight turns or low ceilings
- [ ] Doorway widths assessed for large furniture
- [ ] Destination address confirmed (if different from pickup) and any access notes noted

**Customer expectations set:**
- [ ] Confirm which room each piece of furniture goes to at the destination
- [ ] Confirm packing scope if packing is included
- [ ] Estimated completion time communicated (not promised)

---

## During the Job — Standards

**Loading:**
- All furniture wrapped in moving blankets before being moved — not in the truck
- Legs, corners, and hardware attachment points specifically protected
- Heavy items loaded first: appliances, bed frames, dressers, bookcases
- Fragile items loaded last, cushioned, and marked with crew so they come off first
- Never stack heavy boxes on top of fragile items
- Furniture standing upright where possible (especially sofas, mattresses)
- Strategic placement: use every cubic foot; no wasted space

**Packing (if in scope):**
- Each item individually wrapped — no loose items in boxes
- Dishes and plates edge-stacked (never flat)
- Glassware individually wrapped with paper stuffed inside each glass
- Box contents labeled on top and two sides
- "Fragile" + "This Side Up" marked clearly in large letters on fragile boxes
- Correct box weight: no box heavier than 50 lbs; prefer 35 lbs for all-day carries
- All open space in boxes filled with crumpled paper — no movement when shaken

**Furniture disassembly (if in scope):**
- Hardware bagged by component (one bag per connection point, not one bag per piece)
- Each bag labeled with the furniture piece and component
- Photo taken of assembled piece before disassembly on complex items
- Panels leaned vertically against wall with blanket between them — not flat on floor

**Furniture assembly (if in scope):**
- Reverse the disassembly order
- All fasteners hand-tightened before using a tool on any of them
- Square checked before final tightening
- Nothing forced — if it doesn't go together easily, stop and check orientation
- Assembly completed in the room where the furniture belongs (not assembled then moved)

---

## End-of-Job Walkthrough

Complete this with the customer present before calling the job done.

**At origin (if loading/packing):**
- [ ] Every room walked — nothing left behind that should have moved
- [ ] All closets, under beds, attic/garage/storage areas confirmed
- [ ] Nothing left on counters, in cabinets, or behind doors that should have been packed
- [ ] No damage to walls, floors, or doorframes during loading (note and photo any damage found)
- [ ] All packing debris (boxes, paper, tape) removed or stacked per customer preference

**At destination (if unloading/assembly):**
- [ ] Every box in the correct labeled room
- [ ] All furniture in the correct room — customer confirms placement
- [ ] All assembled furniture confirmed structurally sound and level
- [ ] Fragile items confirmed undamaged
- [ ] Nothing left in the truck/container that should have been unloaded
- [ ] No damage to new home's walls, floors, or doorframes (note and photo any found)

**Customer sign-off:**
- [ ] Customer walked through with crew — all items confirmed
- [ ] Customer satisfied with the job
- [ ] Any open items noted in writing before leaving (e.g., "Customer to call back re: missing box — crew confirmed not on truck")

---

## Payment Collection

- [ ] Invoice amount confirmed verbally with customer
- [ ] Payment collected before crew leaves
- [ ] Receipt provided (photo of written invoice or digital invoice)
- [ ] Payment method noted in job log

**If customer requests to pay later:** Do not leave without written confirmation of payment agreement and a scheduled payment date. Verbal agreements after the crew leaves rarely result in prompt payment.

---

## After the Job — Crew Responsibilities

- [ ] All equipment returned to the work vehicle
- [ ] No customer items left in the vehicle — do a cargo check
- [ ] Moving blankets and shrink wrap remnants collected
- [ ] Job end time noted (for accurate hourly billing)
- [ ] Any damage, incidents, or unusual situations reported to the owner same day — in writing (text is fine)
- [ ] Customer review card or verbal review request delivered

---

## Damage Reporting Protocol

If any item is damaged or any property (wall, floor, door) is damaged during the job:

1. **Stop.** Do not continue moving other items until the damage is documented.
2. **Photograph** the damage from multiple angles before moving anything further.
3. **Inform the customer immediately** — calmly and directly. Do not minimize or deflect.
4. **Text the owner:** "Damage at [customer name] job — [brief description]. Photos sent."
5. **Document in writing** with the customer: what was damaged, how, and that it was noted.
6. **Do not make promises** about repair, replacement, or compensation — that decision belongs to the owner.
7. **Complete the job** (unless the customer asks you to stop) and complete the end-of-job walkthrough on everything else.

The goal is honesty, documentation, and escalation — not minimization.
