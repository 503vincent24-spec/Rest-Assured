and the surrounding area, including Keizer, Woodburn, Albany, McMinnville,
Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, and Portland Metro. Our crew handles loading,

FAQs (Google Q&A — seed these yourself so accurate answers appear first,
since anyone can post a question publicly)
================================================================================
Q: Do you provide the moving truck?
A: No — we're a moving labor company. We handle loading, unloading, packing,
   unpacking, and furniture assembly. You arrange your own truck, trailer,
   POD, or moving container.

Q: Can I hire you for just loading or just unloading?
A: Yes. Many customers book us for only one end of their move. You can also
   book both.

Q: How do you price a job?
A: Flat hourly rate based on crew size and job scope, quoted before work
   begins — no fuel charges, mileage fees, or surprise add-ons.

Q: What areas do you serve?
A: Salem and the surrounding area, including Keizer, Woodburn, Albany,
   McMinnville, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, and Portland Metro.

Q: Do you assemble furniture even if I'm not doing a full move?
A: Yes — furniture assembly and disassembly are available as standalone
   services, no full move required.

Q: How far in advance should I book?
A: 1–2 weeks ahead is ideal, especially around the 1st and last weekend of
   the month. Shorter-notice requests are accommodated when our schedule
   allows.
