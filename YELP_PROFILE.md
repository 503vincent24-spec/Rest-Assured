# Yelp Business Page — Rest Assured Moving LLC

Everything needed to claim, fill out, and run the Yelp page. Each field
below matches a field in Yelp for Business, so you can copy and paste.

> ⚠️ **Yelp is different from Google: never ask customers for Yelp reviews.**
> Yelp's guidelines forbid asking for reviews, even politely and even from
> happy customers. Asked-for reviews tend to get filtered into "not currently
> recommended", and repeated asking can put a public *Consumer Alert* on the
> page. Ask for **Google** reviews (see `REVIEW_MANAGEMENT_SYSTEM.md`) and
> let Yelp reviews come on their own. Section 6 shows what you *can* do.

## 1. Claim the Page (about 15 minutes)

1. Go to **biz.yelp.com** and choose **Claim your business for free**.
2. Search for "Rest Assured Moving". If nothing comes up, choose
   **Add your business**.
3. Use `Shawn@restassuredmoving.net` as the login and turn on two-step
   verification.
4. Verify by **phone call or text** to (971) 302-0120.
5. **Address:** we work at customers' homes, not a storefront. Enter the
   business address, then tick **"I deliver goods and services to my
   customers"** (or the "hide my address" option, depending on the version
   Yelp shows you). This hides the street address and shows a service area
   instead.
6. Ignore the ad offers during sign-up. Section 7 explains when Yelp Ads
   are worth paying for.

## 5. Request-a-Quote & Messages

Yelp shows each business's **response time** and **response rate** on its
page, and fast responders get more quote requests.

- Turn on **Request a Quote** and the Yelp for Business app's push
  notifications.
- Reply within **15 minutes** during business hours. Saved reply:

```
Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to Rest Assured Moving! Happy to help
with your move. To give you an exact quote, could you share:
1) Move date (and whether it's flexible)
2) Home size and any stairs/elevators
3) The truck, PODS, or container you're using
4) Pickup and drop-off cities
Our rates are $95/hr for 2 movers and $130/hr for 3 (2-hour minimum),
blankets and wrap included, no fuel or mileage fees. You can also call
or text Shawn directly at (971) 302-0120.
```

- **If they need a truck too:** say plainly that we're labor-only and
  suggest they book a U-Haul, Penske, or PODS. Many will book that and
  then hire us.

## 6. Reviews: What's Allowed

| ✅ Allowed | ❌ Not allowed |
|---|---|
| A "Find us on Yelp" sticker or badge on the website or in an email footer | "Please leave us a review on Yelp" in any form |
| Replying publicly to every review, good or bad | Discounts or freebies in exchange for reviews |
| A private message to a reviewer to fix a problem | Asking only happy customers to post |
| Reporting reviews that break Yelp's rules (with **Report review**) | Reviews from friends, family, or employees |

**Reply template, positive review:**
```
Thank you, [Name]! It was a pleasure helping with your move to [city].
Glad the [specific detail they mentioned] went smoothly. Rest assured,
we're here whenever you need us again. — Shawn
```

**Reply template, negative review:** use the calm, own-it,
take-it-offline wording in `REVIEW_MANAGEMENT_SYSTEM.md` §6. Never argue
in public. Prospective customers judge the *reply* more than the
complaint.

## 7. Yelp Ads (later, not now)

Yelp's sales team will call. Hold off until **all three** are true:

1. You have 5+ Yelp reviews averaging 4.5★ or higher.
2. Google Business Profile and Google Local Services Ads are already
   running (they usually return more per dollar for movers).
3. You can answer leads within 15 minutes.

When you do start: set a fixed **monthly budget** (start at $150–$300),
target only the service-area cities, and track results with the
`utm_campaign=yelp-ads` tag. Cancel after 60 days if cost per *booked job*
is more than about 25% of the average job's value.
