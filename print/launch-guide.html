Phase 1

Put the website online

Time: about 1 hour
Plus up to 1 hour waiting

Your website is finished and stored on GitHub, but it isn't public yet. This phase makes it public at your own web address. All the work is saved on a branch named ccr-60a728e4-birtu0. “Merging” copies it into main, the version the public site is built from.

Step 1.1 · Choose your web address

Your email is on restassuredmoving.net, so you likely already own that domain. Using it for the website is best: customers see one name everywhere.

  • Confirm you own restassuredmoving.net (look for it at the company that runs your email or where you bought it: GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google, Squarespace…).
  • If you don't have a domain yet, you can start on the free GitHub address 503vincent24-spec.github.io/Rest-Assured and switch later.

Step 1.2 · Put your web address into the site

Many files hold a placeholder ([YOUR-DOMAIN], [WEBSITE URL]) where your address goes. One command fills all of them.

Ask Claude

Use the address you'll keep. Once filled in, the placeholders are gone. Switching later means a find-and-replace (Claude can do that too).

Step 1.3 · Merge the work into main

  1. Go to github.com/503vincent24-spec/Rest-Assured and sign in.
  2. Click the Pull requests tab → New pull request.
  3. Set base: main and compare: ccr-60a728e4-birtu0. GitHub should say “Able to merge.”
  4. Click Create pull request, give it a title like “Launch website”, and create it.
  5. Click Merge pull request → Confirm merge.
Or ask Claude

“Open a pull request from the branch into main.” Then you only click Merge.

Step 1.4 · Turn on GitHub Pages (free hosting)

  1. In the repository, click Settings → Pages (left menu).
  2. Under Build and deployment, set Source to Deploy from a branch, Branch to main, folder / (root). Click Save.
  3. Wait 1–10 minutes, then open https://503vincent24-spec.github.io/Rest-Assured/. You should see your homepage.

Phase 2

Turn on lead delivery

Time: 15 minutes
Do it right after Phase 1

The quote and contact forms send leads to Shawn@restassuredmoving.net through a free service called FormSubmit. It needs a one-time “Activate” click. Until you click it, no website leads reach you.

Step 2.1 · Activate

  1. On the live site, open Get a Free Quote and send a test request with your own name and phone. Write “TEST” in the notes.
  2. Check Shawn@restassuredmoving.net for an email from FormSubmit (look in Spam and Promotions too). Click Activate Form.
  3. Send a second test quote. It should arrive as a neat table, and the site should show the thank-you page.
  4. Send one test from the Contact page as well.
What each lead email shows

Name, phone, email, best time to call, services, move date, home size, truck status, addresses, notes, the online estimate they saw, Full Wrap or same-day if chosen, “how did you hear about us”, and the lead source (which ad or site sent them).

Recommended · Ask Claude

If you change the web address later, send another test. FormSubmit may ask you to activate again.

Step 2.2 · Make sure you never miss a lead

  • Turn on email notifications on your phone for Shawn@restassuredmoving.net.
  • Save (971) 302-0120 voicemail with your business greeting: “You've reached Rest Assured Moving. Text this number for the fastest reply.”

Step 2.3 · Tell Google about the site

  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console → Add property → enter restassuredmoving.net (Domain) and follow the verify step. It adds one TXT record at your DNS. That record is safe; it doesn't affect email.

Google reviews it in 2–7 business days

Step 3.2 · Verify (usually a short video)

Google picks the method. Today it's usually a video recording, sometimes a text code, email, or live video call.

Have ready: a branded shirt or the printed business cards (print/business-card.pdf), your blankets, dollies and straps, and your Oregon LLC registration (on screen or printed).

Record one continuous 30–60 second clip, no edits, in this order:

  1. A Salem street sign or landmark, then the outside of where you keep your equipment.
  2. Your equipment and branding: blankets, dollies, straps, shirt, business cards.
  3. Unlock your garage or storage unit with your own key, and show the LLC registration with the business name visible.

Upload it in the Google Business Profile app. Don't film customers' homes or faces, and don't show a rental truck as if it's yours.

If it's rejected

Fix what Google names (usually extra words in the name, a website that isn't live, or a phone that doesn't match), wait 24 hours, and click Get verified again. Never create a second profile; duplicates get both suspended.

Step 3.3 · The day you're approved

  • Review link: Profile → Ask for reviews → copy the link. Ask Claude: “Here's my Google review link: … put it everywhere.”
  • Photos: upload the logo assets/social/profile-1000.png, the cover assets/photos/upload/job-full-wrap.jpg, and all 8 photos in assets/photos/upload/.
  • Turn on Messages and the quote button (link to your quote page).
  • Publish the first post from GBP_CONTENT.md. Aim for one post a week.
  • Bing Places (bingplaces.com): import your Google profile. Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Search Console. ChatGPT and Copilot search use Bing.
  • Apple Business Connect (businessconnect.apple.com): same name, phone, hours, area. Puts you in Apple Maps and Siri.

Skip Google Local Services Ads for now. They require proof of liability insurance.

Phase 5

Start getting jobs

Time: days 2–7
Then ongoing

Step 5.1 · Moving-labor marketplaces (fastest first jobs)

These sites send customers who have already rented a truck and need labor, which is exactly what you sell. The platform asks every customer for a review, so your track record builds fast.

  • U-Haul Moving Help (movinghelp.com → become a provider): background check, ID, bank account.
  • HireAHelper (hireahelper.com → become a mover). Insurance isn't required for labor-only jobs.
  • Optional: TaskRabbit (furniture assembly) and Thumbtack (pay per lead).
  • List as Rest Assured Moving LLC with the same phone and logo. Price a little lower until you have about 10 reviews. Read each platform's fees before your first job.

Step 5.2 · Craigslist

  • Post Ad 1 (Salem) and Ad 5 (Portland) from CRAIGSLIST_ADS.md, with craigslist-ad-1-1200x900.png first, then 2–3 job photos.
  • When an ad is about 48 hours old, press Renew in your account (don't delete and re-post). Keep at most 2 live ads per city. Reply scripts are in the same file.

Step 5.3 · Print and hand out

  • Business cards: upload print/business-card.pdf to Vistaprint, Moo, Staples or a local shop. Standard 3.5 × 2 in, double-sided, matte, 14–16 pt. Order 500. Hand two to every customer.
  • Flyers: print print/flyer-laundromat.pdf and print/flyer-apartments.pdf in color on plain paper, 50 of each. Each QR code tells you which flyer the text came from.
  • Where: laundromats (ask the manager first), apartment leasing offices (around the 20th of the month), U-Haul dealers, storage facilities. Scripts: REFERRAL_PARTNER_TOOLKIT.md.

Step 5.4 · People you know

  • Text 20 people: “I've launched Rest Assured Moving. If you or anyone you know is moving, I'd appreciate the referral.” Usually brings 1–3 leads.
  • Referral reward: $25 off for both the past customer and the new customer. Use the referral cards.
The rule that wins jobs

Answer every call, text, and message within 15 minutes during business hours. In moving labor, the first one to respond usually gets the job.

Phase 6

Run every job

Every lead, every job

1 · A lead comes in

  • Reply within 15 minutes. Ask the 5 questions in SALES_PLAYBOOK.md: move date (flexible?), home size and stairs, which truck or container, which cities and which end(s), and anything to pack or take apart.
  • Note how they found you (the lead email shows it for web leads).

2 · Quote

  • Use the estimator or the cheat sheet on the last page. Say the crew size, hourly rate, and likely range.
  • Offer Full Wrap when they book both ends and have nice furniture, a long drive, a storage unit, or rain coming.
  • Same-day request? Check a crew is truly free before saying yes. Quote $115/hr (2 movers) or $155/hr (3), text the rate and arrival window, and wait for a “yes” by text. If no crew is free, offer tomorrow at the regular rate.

3 · Confirm in writing

  • Send the confirmation email from EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md (rate, crew, date, arrival window).
  • For bigger jobs, send SERVICE_AGREEMENT_TEMPLATE.md and get “I agree” back. Have an Oregon attorney review it once.
  • Day before: send the reminder template and confirm the truck is booked.

4 · Job day

  • Use JOB_SITE_CHECKLIST.md: arrival, walkthrough, wrap everything, load heavy and low, final walkthrough with the customer.
  • The clock runs from arrival to the end-of-job walkthrough. 2-hour minimum, then 30-minute steps.
  • Report any damage to the customer before you leave, and handle it directly.

5 · Get paid and get the review

  • Payment is due at the end: cash, card, Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or PayPal. Use invoice-template.html.
  • Ask for a Google review in person, then send the review email within 24 hours (REVIEW_MANAGEMENT_SYSTEM.md). Never ask for Yelp reviews.
  • Hand over two business cards and a referral card.

6 · Once a month

  • Fill in the lead-tracking table (ONLINE_LISTINGS_LAUNCH_KIT.md §5). Put more time and money into the two sources that book the most jobs.
  • Add new job photos and real reviews (with permission). Ask Claude to add them to the site.
