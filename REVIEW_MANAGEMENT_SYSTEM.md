# Review Management System — Rest Assured Moving LLC

Google reviews are the single highest-ROI marketing activity for a new
local service business. They directly affect search visibility, LSA
performance, click-through rate, and website conversion. Every job is
a review opportunity.

---

## 1. The Core Loop

```
Deliver excellent work → Request review within 24–48 hrs → Respond publicly
```

The window of peak goodwill closes fast. After 72 hours, response rates
drop significantly.

---

## 2. Get Your Direct Review Link First

**Create a one-click Google review link before requesting a single review:**

1. Sign in to Google Business Profile (business.google.com)
2. Click "Get more reviews"
3. Copy the short URL: `https://g.page/r/[UNIQUE-ID]/review`

**Place this link in:**
- Post-Move Follow-Up email (EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md)
- AI knowledge base [REVIEW LINK] placeholder
- Email signature
- Business card or review card
- Thank-you page on the website

Without a direct link, most customers won't leave a review even if they
intended to.

---

## 3. Verbal Request (at job end)

If the customer is visibly satisfied, before leaving:

> "Really glad this worked out — thanks for trusting us with it. If you
> feel like leaving us a Google review, it genuinely helps. Takes about
> 60 seconds. Here's the link."

Hand them a card with the link or text it right there.

**Do not say:** "only if you give us 5 stars" or "only if you had a good
experience" — Google prohibits conditional review solicitation.

If anything went wrong, skip the verbal request. Let the email handle it
after the issue is resolved.

---

## 4. Email Request (24–48 hours after job)

Use the Post-Move Follow-Up Template from EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md.
Key elements: direct link, "takes 60 seconds" framing, single ask.

**If no review after 5 days:** One optional text follow-up:
> "Hey [Name], [Your name] from Rest Assured Moving. Sharing our review
> link in case it's easier on your phone: [LINK]. Thanks again!"

That's the maximum: one email + one text. Do not send more.

| Milestone | Impact |

**Monthly minimum target:** 1 new review per week.
**Peak season target:** 2+ reviews per week.

## 6. Responding to Reviews

**Respond to every review within 48 hours.** This signals to Google that
the business is active, and every response is read by future customers.

### 5-Star Reviews — Response Templates

**Template A (loading/unloading):**
> "Thank you, [Name]! Glad the loading went smoothly and everything arrived
> safely. It was a pleasure working with you — thanks for trusting us with
> your Salem move."

**Template B (assembly):**
> "Thanks so much, [Name]! Furniture assembly is one of our favorites —
> great to hear everything came together cleanly. Hope you're enjoying the
> new setup!"

**Template C (full service):**
> "This is exactly what we aim for on every job. Thank you for the kind
> review, [Name], and for trusting Rest Assured Moving with your Keizer move."

Vary responses — identical replies to multiple reviews look automated and
reduce trust.

### 4-Star Reviews

Respond as you would a 5-star, acknowledge the specific feedback briefly:
> "Thank you, [Name] — glad the overall move went well. Your note about
> [specific thing] is helpful feedback, and we'll use it. Really appreciate
> you taking the time."

### Negative Reviews (1–3 stars)

- Respond within 24 hours
- Never argue or get defensive publicly
- Acknowledge, apologize for the gap, offer to resolve offline

> "Hi [Name], I'm sorry your experience didn't meet the standard we hold
> ourselves to. I'd like to understand what happened and make it right.
> Please contact me directly at [PHONE] / [EMAIL]."

Then contact the customer privately, listen fully, and resolve genuinely.
A resolved complaint updated to 4–5 stars is among the most powerful
social proof a local business can display.

## 7. Review Platform Priority

| Platform | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Google** | #1 — Critical | Directly affects search ranking and LSA performance |
| **Nextdoor** | #2 | Neighborhood-level trust, high weight locally |
| **Facebook** | #3 | Visible on business page |
| **Yelp** | #4 | Secondary; some customers check |

Build Google to 25+ first before actively requesting reviews elsewhere.

## 8. Tracking

Add a review column to your job log:

| Job # | Customer | Date | Requested | Received | Platform | Stars |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RAM-001 | J. Smith | [date] | Yes | Yes | Google | 5 |
| RAM-002 | M. Chen | [date] | Yes | No | — | — |

**Monthly metric:** review conversion rate = reviews received ÷ reviews
requested. Target 25%+. Below 20%: revisit timing and message directness.

---

## 9. What's Prohibited (Google Policy)

- Incentivizing reviews ("leave a 5-star review for 10% off")
- Discouraging negative reviews ("only review us if you're happy")
- Posting fake reviews
- Buying reviews from any service

Violations can result in all reviews being removed or account suspension.
The system in this document complies fully with current Google policy.
