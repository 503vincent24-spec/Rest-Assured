## 7. Q&A — Seed These Questions Yourself
Post these as questions yourself (logged into the business profile) and immediately answer them. This prevents inaccurate public answers and ensures the right information appears prominently.

**Q: Do you provide the moving truck?**
A: No. Rest Assured Moving LLC is a moving labor company, not a transportation company. We provide the crew — you arrange your own truck rental, trailer, PODS, or moving container. We handle the loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly around whatever you've arranged.

**Q: Can I hire you for just loading, or just unloading?**
A: Yes. You can book any single service — loading only, unloading only, packing, unpacking, or furniture assembly. We don't require you to book all services.

**Q: How is pricing structured?**
A: Flat hourly rate based on crew size and job scope, confirmed before work begins. No fuel surcharges, no mileage fees, no hidden add-ons. Your invoice reflects the actual hours worked at the agreed rate.

**Q: Do you assemble furniture without a full move?**
A: Yes. Furniture assembly and disassembly are available as completely standalone services. No move required.

**Q: How far in advance should I book?**
A: We recommend 1–2 weeks ahead, especially around the 1st and last weekend of the month. We accommodate shorter-notice requests when the schedule allows.

**Q: What areas do you serve?**
A: Salem, Keizer, Woodburn, Albany, McMinnville, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, and the greater Portland Metro area.

**Q: Are you licensed and insured?**
A: Yes. Rest Assured Moving LLC is a licensed business and carries appropriate liability insurance. Contact us for documentation if needed.

## 9. Photo Upload Checklist
GBP listings with 10+ photos receive significantly more engagement. Upload in this priority order:

**Immediate (before launch):**
- [ ] Logo (square crop, minimum 720×720px PNG — export from logo-mark.svg)
- [ ] Cover photo (crew working — loading, wrapping furniture, or in front of a clean exterior)

**First 30 days:**
- [ ] 3–5 "crew at work" action photos (loading, packing, assembly)
- [ ] 1 "team" or "owner" photo
- [ ] 1 before/after of a furniture assembly job

**Ongoing:**
- [ ] Add photos of completed jobs (with customer permission)
- [ ] Seasonal content (summer move season, etc.)
- [ ] Interior photos of any service vehicles or equipment (optional)

**Photo guidelines:**
- Minimum 720px on shorter side; 1080px+ preferred
- Real photos outperform stock photos dramatically for local GBP
- Never use truck imagery that implies transportation services
- Bright, natural light performs better than dark interiors
