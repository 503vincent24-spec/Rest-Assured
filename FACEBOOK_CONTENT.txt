ABOUT SECTION (long-form "Our Story" / About tab)
================================================================================
Rest Assured Moving LLC is a Salem, Oregon-based moving labor company. We
specialize in the part of moving day that has nothing to do with driving a
truck and everything to do with the actual lifting, wrapping, packing, and
assembling — loading and unloading your rental truck, trailer, POD, or
moving container; packing and unpacking your boxes; and assembling or
disassembling furniture so it survives the trip and goes back together
correctly on the other end.

We don't operate moving trucks or provide transportation — you choose how
your belongings get from point A to point B. What we provide is the trained,
careful crew that handles everything around that: protecting your furniture,
maximizing your truck's space, and making sure nothing gets left behind.

We serve Salem and the surrounding communities, including Keizer, Woodburn,
Albany, McMinnville, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, and the Portland Metro
area. Flat hourly pricing, no fuel surcharges, no hidden fees — just a crew
that shows up, works the checklist, and gets it done right.

POST 2 — Service education: Loading
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Loading a truck isn't just "put boxes in until it's full." Our crew plans the
load before a single box goes in — heaviest items low and toward the cab,
fragile items cushioned and protected, and every cubic foot used so you're
not paying for a second trip. That's the difference moving labor makes.

POST 10 — Tip (seasonal-neutral, evergreen)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Measure twice, move once: before moving day, measure your largest furniture
AND the doorways/stairwells at both addresses. A couch that fit through one
doorway won't necessarily fit through another. Five minutes with a tape
measure saves an hour of hallway gymnastics. 📏
