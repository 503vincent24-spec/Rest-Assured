## Style Notes (apply across all templates)

**Always:**
- Address the customer by first name
- Reference the specific job date and services
- Include a phone number in every email
- Reinforce that the truck/container is the customer's responsibility (Templates 1 & 2)
- Use "crew" or "movers" — never "drivers"

**Never:**
- Imply we drive or transport belongings
- Use generic phrases like "stress-free move" without backing them up
- Leave the labor-only model unclear to new customers

**Sign-off:** The closing line "Rest assured, it's handled." can be added to any template and is especially effective in confirmation and reminder emails.
