10 Tips for Preparing for Moving Day

By the Rest Assured Moving crew · Salem, OR

Most moving-day stress doesn't come from the move itself — it comes from everything that wasn't decided ahead of time. Whether you're loading a rental truck, a POD, or a trailer, these ten steps will get you to moving day actually ready for it.

1. Lock in your truck or container at least two weeks out

Truck rentals, PODS, and moving containers get booked up fast, especially around the 1st and last weekend of the month. Reserve yours as early as possible, and confirm the size matches your home — oversizing by one tier is usually cheaper than running out of space halfway through loading.

2. Book your moving labor before you book the date you actually want

If you're hiring a crew for loading and unloading services, popular dates fill first. Lock in your labor at the same time you lock in your truck, not after.

3. Start a "keep, donate, toss" pass three weeks out

Every item you move costs time and space. Walk each room and sort honestly — it's far easier to do this before boxes are sealed than to discover an unused item again in your new kitchen.

4. Collect free boxes early, buy specialty boxes once

Liquor stores, bookstores, and grocery stores often give away sturdy boxes for free. Save your money for the boxes that actually need to be purpose-built: dish-pack boxes, wardrobe boxes, and mirror/picture boxes.

5. Pack a clearly labeled "first night" box

Toiletries, phone chargers, a change of clothes, basic tools, and any medication should travel with you, not in the truck. Label it boldly so it doesn't get loaded with everything else.

6. Label boxes by room, not by category

"Kitchen" and "Master Bedroom" tell your unloading crew exactly where to set a box down. "Misc" tells them nothing, and that's how boxes end up stacked in the garage for a month.

7. Disassemble large furniture the day before, not the morning of

Bed frames, large bookshelves, and sectionals almost always need to come apart to fit through doorways or maximize truck space. Doing this the night before — and bagging the hardware per piece — saves real time on moving morning. If you'd rather not deal with it, this is exactly what furniture assembly services are for.

8. Measure doorways, stairwells, and elevators at both ends

A couch that fit through one doorway won't necessarily fit through another. A five-minute measuring pass at both addresses prevents a frustrating standoff in a hallway.

9. Clear a straight path before the crew arrives

Move rugs, plants, and stray items out of hallways and doorways ahead of time. A clear path is the single biggest factor in how fast a loading or unloading job goes.

10. Confirm arrival times the day before

Whether it's your truck rental pickup, your moving labor crew, or both, a quick confirmation call the day before avoids any 8am surprises.
