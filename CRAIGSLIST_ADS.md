# Craigslist Ads — Rest Assured Moving LLC

Copy-paste-ready Craigslist ads for the Salem and Portland markets, plus the
posting rules and schedule that keep them live and unflagged.

Craigslist is where people search for **"moving help"** when they've already
rented the truck and need someone to load it. That's exactly what we sell, so
these ads work best when they say "labor only" up front.

## 2. Rules That Get Ads Removed (Avoid These)

1. **No duplicate ads.** Don't post the same ad to both Salem and Portland,
   or to more than one category. Each city gets its own ad with different
   wording (Ads 1 and 5 below are written that way).
2. **Wait 48 hours before reposting** in the same city and category. If you
   repost sooner, the ad gets "ghosted": it still shows in your account but
   nobody else can see it.
3. **Don't stuff keywords.** Ads with long lists of city names or tags at the
   bottom get flagged. The service-area line in each ad below is as long as it
   safely gets.
4. **Never imply transportation.** The category is called "labor / hauling /
   moving", but we only do labor. Every ad says so. That stops the wrong
   leads ("can you move my couch across town?") and keeps us inside the
   brand rule.

## 4. Posting Schedule (4-Week Rotation)

| Day | Salem site | Portland site |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 · Mon | Ad 1 (Main) | Ad 5 (Portland) |
| Week 1 · Thu | Ad 2 (Apartments) — different title, so allowed | — |
| Week 2 · Mon | Renew Ad 1 | Renew Ad 5 |
| Week 2 · Thu | Ad 3 (Containers) | — |
| Week 3 · Mon | Renew Ad 1 | Renew Ad 5 |
| Week 3 · Thu | Ad 4 (Assembly) | — |
| Week 4 · Mon | Delete the weakest ad and repost Ad 1 with fresh photos | Renew Ad 5 |

- **Best posting times:** Sunday evening and Monday morning, when people plan
  the week's move. Move volume peaks in the last and first days of each month,
  so post again around the 20th–25th.
- **Renewing** bumps an ad back to the top once it is about 48 hours old. Use
  the *renew* button in your account. Don't delete and re-post.
- **Keep at most 2 live ads per city.** More than that tends to get flagged.

## 5. Replying to Craigslist Leads

Speed wins on Craigslist. The first mover to answer usually gets the job.

**Text reply (within 15 minutes):**
```
Hi, this is Shawn with Rest Assured Moving — thanks for reaching out!
To give you an exact quote I just need: (1) move date, (2) size of home
and floor/stairs, (3) the truck or container you're using, and (4) the
pickup and drop-off cities. Our rate is $95/hr for 2 movers, $130/hr
for 3, with a 2-hour minimum. Want me to give you a quick call?
```

**If they ask "do you have a truck?":**
```
We're a labor-only crew, so we don't provide the truck. That keeps our
rate lower. Most customers book a U-Haul, Penske, or Budget truck, or
a PODS container, and we handle all the loading and unloading. Happy to
suggest a truck size for your home if that helps!
```

**Screening:** ignore anyone who offers to pay by check for more than the
quote and asks for the difference back, wants you to use a "shipping
agent", or won't give an address. Those are scams. Take payment only by
the methods in the FAQ (cash, Cash App, Zelle, Venmo, card, PayPal), on or
after the job.
