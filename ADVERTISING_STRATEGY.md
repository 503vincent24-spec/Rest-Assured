# Advertising Strategy — Rest Assured Moving LLC

A channel-by-channel paid advertising plan, sequenced by priority and budget
tier, with sample ad copy, targeting notes, and tracking setup. This assumes
no paid advertising is currently running — start with Tier 1 and expand as
budget and lead volume justify it.

================================================================================
IMPORTANT — READ BEFORE LAUNCHING GOOGLE ADS
================================================================================
Google requires moving companies to go through a **mover verification
program** before running ads that reference moving services, due to a history
of fraud in the industry. This has historically applied broadly to "moving
companies" as a category — it is worth confirming directly with Google
whether a labor-only company (no trucks, no transportation) is in scope for
this requirement or exempt from it, since the policy was originally built
around preventing rogue interstate movers/brokers, not labor-only crews.
**Do not assume either way — verify directly in Google Ads' policy center or
with Google Ads support before building campaigns**, since running afoul of
this can get an account suspended before it ever serves an impression. This
is the single most important action item in this document.

================================================================================
SECTION 1 — CHANNEL PRIORITY & RATIONALE
================================================================================

| Priority | Channel | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google Business Profile (organic, already set up) | Free, highest-intent traffic for local "moving labor near me" searches. Foundation for everything else. |
| 2 | Google Local Services Ads (LSA) | Pay-per-lead (not per-click), built specifically for local service businesses, includes a "Google Screened" trust badge. Often the best ROI starting point for a local labor business — but confirm mover-category verification requirements first. |
| 3 | Google Search Ads (traditional PPC) | Highest commercial intent ("moving help Salem Oregon"), but more expensive per click and requires the verification check above. |
| 4 | Facebook & Instagram Ads | Good for awareness, retargeting, and seasonal campaigns; lower intent than search but much cheaper reach. |
| 5 | Nextdoor | Hyper-local, strong word-of-mouth fit, relatively low cost. |
| 6 | Local partnerships/sponsorships | Not "ads" in the traditional sense, but often the best cost-per-lead for a business this size (see SOCIAL_MEDIA_CAMPAIGN_STRATEGY.md Section 5). |

================================================================================
SECTION 2 — BUDGET TIERS (Monthly, illustrative starting points)
================================================================================

**Tier 1 — Starter ($300–$600/month)**
- Google Local Services Ads: $250–$450/month (pay per lead, set a weekly cap)
- Facebook/Instagram boosted posts: $50–$150/month (boost top-performing
  organic posts rather than building full campaigns yet)

**Tier 2 — Growth ($800–$1,500/month)**
- Google Local Services Ads: $400–$600/month
- Google Search Ads: $300–$600/month (narrow keyword list, see Section 3)
- Facebook/Instagram Ads: $150–$300/month (proper campaign structure, see
  Section 4)

**Tier 3 — Established ($2,000+/month)**
- All of the above, scaled up
- Nextdoor local ads: $200–$400/month
- Retargeting campaigns (website visitors who didn't request a quote)
- Seasonal spend increases during summer moving season (May–August)

Recommendation: start at Tier 1, track cost-per-lead for 60–90 days, then
reallocate budget toward whichever channel produces the cheapest qualified
leads before scaling to Tier 2.

SECTION 3 — GOOGLE SEARCH ADS: KEYWORDS & SAMPLE COPY
================================================================================

### Keyword themes to bid on
- moving labor Salem Oregon
- moving help Salem OR
- loading and unloading services
- packing and unpacking services
- furniture assembly Salem
- moving crew for rental truck
- POD loading help
- hire movers to load truck
- furniture disassembly service

### Negative keywords (important — exclude these to avoid wasted spend)
- "moving company" (alone, without "labor" or "help" — too likely to attract
  full-service-move searchers who actually need transportation)
- "long distance movers"
- "moving truck rental" / "truck rental"
- "interstate movers"
- "cheap movers" (tends to attract price-shoppers expecting full-service
  pricing far below what labor-only/hourly pricing looks like)
- free

SECTION 4 — FACEBOOK / INSTAGRAM ADS
================================================================================

### Campaign structure recommendation
- **Campaign objective:** Leads (using Meta's lead form) or Traffic (to the
  contact page) — test both, leads forms tend to convert more but can be
  lower-intent than someone who clicks through to the actual site.
- **Audience targeting:**
  - Location: Salem, Keizer, Woodburn, Albany, McMinnville, Beaverton,
    Hillsboro, Tigard, Portland Metro (radius or city-list targeting)
  - Age: 25–55 (primary renter/homeowner moving demographic)
  - Interests/behaviors: "Recently moved," "Home improvement," "Moving and
    relocation," real estate browsing behavior, apartment-hunting signals
  - Life events: "Recently moved" is a literal Meta life-event targeting
    option — use it
- **Creative formats:** single image, carousel (service breakdown), and
  short-form video (Reels-style placement) all worth testing.

### Sample Ad Concepts

**Ad 1 — Awareness / Service Education**
Headline: You bring the truck. We bring the crew.
Primary text: Loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly
in Salem, Oregon. Flat hourly rate, no hidden fees. Free quotes.
CTA button: Get Quote
Image idea: crew loading a truck, or the manifest-card graphic from the
website hero.

**Ad 2 — Offer / Direct Response**
Headline: Free Moving Labor Quotes — Salem, OR
Primary text: Tell us your move date and what you need handled — we'll send
a flat hourly quote, usually within one business day.
CTA button: Get Quote
Image idea: simple, bold text-on-color graphic (easy to produce without
photography — could be built as a quick Canva or Visualizer graphic).

**Ad 3 — Retargeting (website visitors who didn't convert)**
Headline: Still need help with your move?
Primary text: You checked us out — here's a free, no-obligation quote
whenever you're ready. Loading, unloading, packing & furniture assembly.
CTA button: Get Quote
Audience: Website Custom Audience, last 30 days, excluding people who already
submitted the contact form (requires Meta Pixel installed on the site).

SECTION 5 — NEXTDOOR
================================================================================
- Claim/verify the Nextdoor Business Page for Rest Assured Moving LLC.
- Nextdoor Local Deals or Neighborhood Sponsorship ads can target by specific
  Salem-area neighborhoods — useful for hyper-local seasonal pushes (e.g.,
  apartment-heavy neighborhoods during August turnover).
- Encourage satisfied customers to recommend the business directly in their
  neighborhood feed — this carries outsized weight on Nextdoor compared to
  other platforms, since recommendations there read as genuine neighbor-to-
  neighbor advice.

SECTION 6 — TRACKING & MEASUREMENT SETUP
================================================================================

**Before spending on any paid channel, set these up:**

1. **Call tracking number** — use a tracking number (via Google Local
   Services Ads' built-in tracking, CallRail, or similar) specifically for
   paid channels, separate from the organic/website number, so you can
   attribute phone leads to ad spend accurately.
2. **UTM parameters** on every paid link, e.g.:
   `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=summer2026`
   This requires basic analytics on the website (Google Analytics 4 or
   similar) to actually read these parameters — confirm this is installed.
3. **Meta Pixel** installed on the website (required for Facebook/Instagram
   retargeting in Section 4, Ad 3).
4. **Google Ads Conversion Tracking** linked to the contact form submission
   and/or phone call clicks.
5. **A single source-of-truth lead log** — even a simple spreadsheet with
   columns for Date, Lead Source, Service Requested, Booked? (Y/N), Job
   Value — this is what actually tells you which channel is worth scaling,
   more reliably than any platform's self-reported numbers.

**Core KPIs to review monthly:**
- Cost per lead, by channel
- Lead-to-booking conversion rate, by channel
- Cost per booked job, by channel (the number that actually matters)
- Organic vs. paid lead mix (to sanity-check whether paid spend is additive
  or just capturing demand that would have come organically anyway)

================================================================================
SECTION 7 — SEQUENCING RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================
1. Confirm Google's mover-ads verification requirement (see top of document)
   before building any Google campaign.
2. Set up tracking (Section 6) before spending a dollar on ads — you cannot
   retroactively measure what you didn't track.
3. Launch Google Local Services Ads first (lowest setup complexity, pay-per-
   lead model reduces risk of wasted spend on bad clicks).
4. Layer in Facebook/Instagram awareness + retargeting once the website has
   enough traffic for the Pixel to build a useful retargeting audience
   (roughly 100+ monthly visitors is a reasonable floor).
5. Add Google Search Ads once Local Services Ads' lead volume plateaus and
   you want to capture additional search volume.
6. Add Nextdoor and increase seasonal spend ahead of summer moving season.
