# Referral Partner Toolkit — Rest Assured Moving LLC

Local partnerships with truck rental companies, real estate agents, and
apartment managers are the highest-ROI marketing activity that costs nothing
but time. A single well-placed referral relationship can produce 20–30 warm
leads per year — customers who arrive pre-sold because a trusted source
recommended you.

This toolkit covers who to approach, how to approach them, and what to say.

---

## 1. Partner Types by Priority

| Partner type | Why they refer | What you offer | Estimated leads/year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truck rental dealers | Customers ask "know anyone who can help load this?" constantly | Easy answer to their most common customer question | 15–40 |
| Real estate agents | Buyers and sellers always need moving help | Referral reciprocity; they recommend you, you recommend them | 8–20 |
| Apartment complexes / property managers | Move-in/move-out is a known pain point for residents | Convenience for tenants; optional commission arrangement | 5–15 |
| Storage facilities | Customers moving items into storage need loading help | Same labor-only model fits their customers exactly | 5–12 |
| PODS / moving container dealers | Same dynamic as truck rental | Natural referral since they provide the container, not the labor | 10–25 |

Total potential: **40–110 warm leads per year** from 5–10 active partner relationships.

## 2. Truck Rental Companies (Highest Priority)

**Target locations in Salem area:**
- U-Haul (search uhaul.com for local dealers in Salem, Keizer, and Woodburn)
- Penske (penske.com/truck-rental/locations)
- Budget (budgettruck.com/truck-rental/locations)
- Home Depot truck rental (hourly rentals — different use case but same customer)
- Local independent rental yards (call the chamber for a list)

**Why this works:**
Every day, truck rental customers ask the person behind the counter some version
of "do you know anyone who can help me load this?" The counter staff have no
good answer right now. You are that answer.

**The approach:**
Walk in during a non-busy time (Tuesday–Thursday, mid-morning works best).
Ask for the manager or owner — not the front desk staff alone.

**In-person script:**
> "Hi, I'm [NAME] from Rest Assured Moving LLC — we're a local moving labor
> company, meaning we help customers load and unload their rental trucks.
> I imagine you get asked all the time if you know anyone who can help with
> the lifting. I wanted to introduce myself and see if we could be your
> go-to recommendation. We're licensed, insured, and flat hourly pricing —
> no surprises."

**Leave behind:**
- A stack of 20–30 business cards
- 2–3 printed flyers (flyer.html from marketing-collateral)
- Your direct cell number written on the back of a card for the manager

**The ask:**
> "If customers ask for loading help, could you point them our way? We'd
> really appreciate it — and I'll make sure to return the favor whenever
> someone asks us about truck rentals."

**Follow-up cadence:**
- 1 week after initial visit: brief thank-you text to the manager
- 1 month later: stop by with coffee and fresh cards to replenish
- Quarterly: maintain the relationship with a quick check-in

## 3. Real Estate Agents

**Target agents:** Active in Salem, Keizer, Beaverton, Hillsboro, and Tigard.
Focus on agents who handle residential transactions — not commercial.

**How to find them:**
- zillow.com/agent-finder
- realtor.com → find agents in Salem, OR
- Salem Association of Realtors (salemrealtors.com) — member directory
- Ask satisfied customers if they used an agent and could introduce you

**The referral pitch for agents:**

**Email approach:**
```
Subject: Moving labor referral partnership — Rest Assured Moving LLC

Hi [AGENT NAME],

I'm [YOUR NAME], owner of Rest Assured Moving LLC in Salem. We provide
loading, unloading, packing, and furniture assembly for residential moves —
labor only, no trucks.

Your clients are often the people who need exactly this. I'd love to be
a resource you can offer buyers and sellers who ask about moving help.

In return, I'm happy to recommend you to our customers who mention they
need an agent. We work with a lot of people in transition.

No formal arrangement needed — just a conversation to see if it makes sense.
Could we connect for 15 minutes this week?

[YOUR NAME]
Rest Assured Moving LLC
(971) 302-0120
Shawn@restassuredmoving.net
[WEBSITE URL]
```

**What agents value:**
- A trusted referral they can give clients (makes them look more helpful)
- Reciprocal referrals back to their business
- A reliable company that won't embarrass them with a bad experience

**What to offer:**
- Your business card to put in their client welcome packets
- A "preferred vendor" placement on their website (just ask — many say yes)
- Reciprocal referrals when your customers need an agent
- A one-page "moving preparation guide" they can give clients (use content
  from blog-preparing-for-moving-day.html, simplified to a handout)

## 4. Apartment Complexes and Property Managers

**Target in Salem and Keizer:**
Move-in/move-out season (August–September for lease turnovers, year-round for
monthly rentals) creates consistent loading/unloading demand from residents
who don't own trucks and need short-notice help.

**How to approach:**
Call the leasing office and ask for the property manager (not the leasing agent).

**Phone script:**
> "Hi, I'm [NAME] from Rest Assured Moving LLC — we're a local moving labor
> company that helps residents with loading, unloading, and furniture assembly.
> I wanted to introduce ourselves and see if you'd be open to having our
> information available for residents who ask about moving help. We're fully
> insured and local."

**Offer to property managers:**
- A card stack or small flyer stack for the leasing office
- Your business listed in their "move-in resources" packet (many complexes
  have one — ask if they'd add you)
- Option: offer a small resident discount ($10–15 off first job) exclusively
  for their residents — gives the manager something tangible to offer

**Best time to approach:** August–September (peak turnover) is too late to
start the relationship. Approach in March–April so the relationship is warm
by the time move-out season hits.

## 5. PODS and Moving Container Providers

**How it works:** Customers who book a PODS delivery or other moving container
service receive the container — but PODS doesn't send crew to fill it.
The customer is left to figure out the loading themselves.

**Target:** Local PODS dealers and container rental companies.
Search: "PODS Salem Oregon" and "moving container Salem OR" for local contacts.

**The pitch to container companies:**
Same approach as truck rental — walk in, meet the local franchise owner or
manager, introduce yourself as the labor component they don't provide.

**What to offer:**
- Co-referral arrangement (you mention PODS to customers who need containers;
  they mention you to customers who need loading help)
- Your contact information posted at the drop-off/pick-up location if applicable

## 6. Storage Facilities

**Target:** Self-storage locations in Salem, Keizer, and nearby areas.
Search Google Maps for storage facilities in your service area.

**The pitch:**
> "Our customers often move items into storage — and they often need loading
> help to get it there. If you get asked for a labor recommendation, we'd
> love to be your go-to. We're local, licensed, and flat hourly pricing."

**Leave behind:** Cards at the front office. Ask if they'll post a flyer near
the entrance or in the rental paperwork packet.

## 7. Referral Tracking

Track every referral partner and every lead they generate.
Use a simple sheet:

| Partner name | Type | Contact | Date approached | Status | Leads received | Bookings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U-Haul Dallas Hwy | Truck rental | [Manager name, phone] | [Date] | Active | 3 | 2 |
| [Agent name], Salem Realty | Real estate | [email] | [Date] | Pending | 0 | 0 |
| [Complex name], Keizer | Apartment mgr | [phone] | [Date] | Active | 1 | 1 |

Review monthly. Partners who generate zero leads after 3 months may need a
follow-up visit. Partners who generate consistent leads deserve priority
relationship maintenance (drop-in visits, thank-you cards, referrals back).

Ask every new customer: "How did you hear about us?" If they say "the U-Haul
place told me," that's a referral. Record it.

## 8. What NOT to Do

- **Don't pay per-referral commissions to counter staff** at truck rental companies
  without understanding the legal implications. Structured referral fees can
  create issues. Informal reciprocal recommendations between businesses are
  standard practice and don't require formal arrangements.
- **Don't promise exclusivity** ("I'll only recommend your storage facility")
  — you may need to recommend multiple options to serve different customer needs.
- **Don't over-ask** from one partner. If a real estate agent sends you two
  referrals and you send them zero, the relationship won't last.
- **Don't neglect the relationship.** A partner who recommended you 6 months
  ago and hasn't heard from you since will stop recommending you.

---

## 9. Referral Partner Thank-You System

When a partner sends you a booking:
1. **Notify them the same week** — "Just wanted you to know that [name]
   booked with us and mentioned you sent them. Thank you!"
2. **Reciprocate** — actively look for opportunities to send customers their way
3. **Quarterly appreciation** — a coffee drop-off, a thank-you card, or simply
   stopping by to replenish cards and say hello keeps the relationship warm

The partners who refer you consistently are worth more than any advertising
channel. Treat them accordingly.
