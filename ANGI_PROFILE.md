# Angi (formerly Angie's List) — Rest Assured Moving LLC

Angie's List is now **Angi**, and HomeAdvisor's pro network has been merged
into it. Homeowners post a job, and Angi sells that lead to a few local
pros. That makes Angi a **paid lead source**, not a free directory. Set it up
carefully and it books jobs. Set it up loosely and it spends money on
leads that don't fit.

---

## 1. How Angi Charges (read before signing up)

| Cost | What to expect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Membership / sign-up fee | Usually an annual fee in the low hundreds of dollars | Ask the rep for the exact amount and whether it renews automatically |
| Pay per lead | Usually about $15–$80+ per lead for moving-type jobs | Leads are often **shared with 3–4 other pros**. You pay whether or not you win the job |
| Angi Ads (optional) | A monthly budget for top placement in search results | Skip this until per-lead results are proven |

**Before you sign:**
- Get the **exact per-lead price** for each task type you select, in writing
  (email is fine).
- Set a **weekly or monthly lead budget cap**.
- Ask how to request **lead credits** for bad leads, such as a wrong
  service, a fake number, or someone outside the service area. Credits are
  usually possible but have to be requested quickly.
- Confirm you can **pause** leads at any time, for example when the
  schedule is full.

**Break-even math:** if an average job is 3 hours × $95 = $285 and you
book 1 of every 4 leads, each booked job costs 4 × the lead price. At $40 a
lead that's $160, or 56% of the job, which is too high. Aim to win 1 in 2
by answering fast (Section 5), or only pick task types with cheaper leads.

## 2. Sign Up (about 30–45 minutes)

1. Go to **angi.com/pro** (or search "Angi become a pro").
2. Enter the business info exactly as below. It has to match Google, Yelp,
   and Facebook character for character.
3. Complete the **background check** on the owner. Angi requires it, and
   it usually takes a few business days.
4. Upload your business registration (Oregon Secretary of State registry
   number). Skip any optional insurance upload, and never check an
   "insured" box: Rest Assured doesn't carry liability insurance yet.
5. Set up the **Angi Pro app** on your phone with notifications ON.

> **Licensing questions.** Oregon's household-goods *carrier* rules are for
> companies that **transport** belongings. Because we're labor-only, they
> generally don't apply. If Angi asks for a mover license, explain that
> you're labor-only and give your Oregon business registry number. Check
> with ODOT Commerce & Compliance if you're unsure. Never claim a license
> you don't hold.

## 3. Choosing Task Types (the most important step)

Angi sends leads based on the **tasks** you choose. Choose only tasks that
are clearly labor-only. If the task list offers a matching option, use it.

**✅ Turn ON** (exact names vary; pick the closest match):
- Moving labor only / Loading and unloading
- Help loading or unloading a truck, PODS, or container
- Packing and unpacking services
- Furniture assembly
- Furniture disassembly / reassembly
- Moving heavy furniture or items within a home

**❌ Turn OFF:**
- Local moving (full service, where the mover supplies the truck)
- Long-distance / interstate moving
- Junk removal / hauling
- Piano moving, safe moving, pool table moving (specialty equipment)
- Vehicle or boat transport, freight, delivery

**Service area:** list each ZIP code or city: Salem, Keizer, Woodburn,
Albany, McMinnville, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, Portland. Leave out
anything farther away. Out-of-area leads waste the budget.

## 5. Winning the Lead (speed is everything)

Angi leads are shared, and **the first pro to call usually wins**.

- **Call within 5 minutes.** If they don't pick up, text right away:

```
Hi [Name], this is Shawn with Rest Assured Moving — you requested moving
help on Angi. I can give you an exact quote in a 3-minute call. Our rate
is $95/hr for 2 movers ($130/hr for 3), 2-hour minimum, blankets and wrap
included, no fuel or mileage fees. When's a good time to talk?
```

- Follow up once on day 2 and once on day 4, then stop.
- **Wrong-fit lead** (they want a truck, long distance, or a piano): say
  plainly that we're labor-only and suggest they book their truck or
  container first. Then **ask Angi for a lead credit** the same day.

## 6. Reviews on Angi

Unlike Yelp, **Angi lets you ask customers for reviews and encourages it.**
Reviews also raise your position in Angi search and can earn the
*Angi Certified* or *Super Service* style badges.

- Use the review-request tool in the Angi Pro app for every job that came
  from Angi.
- For jobs from other sources, keep asking for **Google** reviews first
  (`REVIEW_MANAGEMENT_SYSTEM.md`). Don't split the asks.
- Answer every Angi review with the reply templates in `YELP_PROFILE.md`
  §6. They work on any platform.

## 7. 60-Day Scorecard: Keep, Fix, or Cancel

Track in the job log (lead source = "Angi"):

| Metric | Target | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Leads received | — | |
| Leads contacted within 5 min | 90%+ | |
| Booked jobs | — | |
| Win rate (booked ÷ leads) | 35%+ | |
| Lead spend + fees | — | |
| **Cost per booked job** | **under $75** | |
| Revenue from Angi jobs | — | |

- **Keep** if cost per booked job is under about 25% of average job revenue.
- **Fix** (narrow the task types, tighten the service area, answer faster)
  if it's 25–40%.
- **Pause or cancel** if it's over 40% after 60 days. Google Business
  Profile, Nextdoor, and Craigslist will likely bring jobs for less.
