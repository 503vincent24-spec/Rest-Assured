Important — read this first

We provide the crew,
not the truck.

Rest Assured Moving LLC is a moving labor company. We do not own or operate moving trucks, and we don't provide transportation between locations. You arrange your own truck rental, trailer, PODS, or moving container — we provide the trained crew to load it, unload it, pack for it, and reassemble on the other end.

This model means you control your transportation costs, and you hire specialists for the physical work. Many customers find the total cost is lower than booking a full-service moving company.

Typical jobs we handle:

  • Loading a U-Haul, Penske, or Budget rental truck
  • Loading or unloading a PODS or moving container
  • Help loading AND unloading — both ends of the move
  • Full or partial packing before moving day
  • Unpacking & settling in after arrival
  • Furniture assembly only — no move required
