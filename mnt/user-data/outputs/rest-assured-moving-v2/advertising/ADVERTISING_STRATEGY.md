## ⚠️ READ FIRST — Google Mover Verification

Google requires moving-related businesses to complete a **Moving Company Verification** program before running ads. This policy was designed to combat fraudulent brokers, but it broadly applies to companies in the moving category. Before building any Google campaign:

1. **Confirm whether this requirement applies** to a labor-only company (no trucks, no transportation) by contacting Google Ads support directly or checking the current policy at ads.google.com/policies.
2. **Do not assume you're exempt.** The policy language has been interpreted broadly.
3. **Attempting to run ads before completing verification can result in account suspension**, which is difficult and slow to reverse.

Complete this verification check before spending a single dollar on Google advertising.

## 1. Channel Priority

| Priority | Channel | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Google Business Profile (free)** | Zero cost; highest-intent local traffic; foundation for everything else. Must be complete and verified before paid ads launch. |
| 2 | **Google Local Services Ads (LSA)** | Pay-per-lead (not per-click); includes Google Screened badge; built for local service businesses; lower risk than traditional PPC. Requires background check verification. |
| 3 | **Google Search Ads** | Highest commercial intent; captures "moving labor Salem Oregon" searches directly. Requires mover verification (see above). |
| 4 | **Facebook & Instagram Ads** | Broader awareness; good for retargeting website visitors; lower intent than search but excellent for seasonal pushes. No mover verification required. |
| 5 | **Nextdoor** | Hyper-local; strong word-of-mouth culture; low cost; high trust factor in residential neighborhoods. |
| 6 | **Local partnerships** | Referral arrangements with truck rental dealers, real estate agents, apartment complexes — zero media cost. See Social Media Campaign Strategy for details. |

## 2. Monthly Budget Tiers

### Tier 1 — Startup ($300–$600/month)
Best for: New business, testing channels before scaling.
- Google Local Services Ads: $200–$400/month (set a weekly lead cap)
- Facebook/Instagram boosted posts: $100–$200/month (boost top-performing organic posts)
- Google Search Ads: Hold until LSA data shows cost-per-lead

### Tier 2 — Growth ($800–$1,500/month)
Best for: Business with proven close rate, ready to scale leads.
- Google LSA: $400–$600/month
- Google Search Ads: $250–$500/month (narrow keyword list, see Section 4)
- Facebook/Instagram Ads: $150–$400/month (proper campaign structure)
- Nextdoor: $0–$100/month (start with free business page, add paid later)

### Tier 3 — Scale ($2,000–$4,000/month)
Best for: Established operation, multiple crews, ready to dominate the market.
- Google LSA: $600–$900/month
- Google Search Ads: $600–$1,200/month (expanded keywords + city-specific campaigns)
- Facebook/Instagram Ads: $400–$900/month (retargeting + prospecting + seasonal)
- Nextdoor: $200–$400/month
- Seasonal multiplier: increase all channels 30–50% in May–August (peak moving season)

**Recommendation:** Start at Tier 1. Run for 60–90 days. Track cost-per-booked-job (not just cost-per-lead) by channel. Allocate Tier 2 budget toward whichever channel produces the lowest cost-per-booking before scaling.

## 3. Google Local Services Ads (LSA)

### Why this first
LSA is pay-per-lead, meaning you pay only when a potential customer contacts you directly through the ad — not when someone clicks through to your website and bounces. For a local labor business, this is a materially lower-risk ad spend than traditional PPC.

### Setup steps
1. Create an account at localservices.google.com
2. Select business category — choose "Moving" or the closest available option
3. Complete Google Screened verification (background check on owner/operators)
4. Confirm mover-category requirements (see top of document)
5. Set weekly budget cap to control spend while testing
6. Add all 9 service cities as target areas
7. Set operating hours to match business hours (Mon–Sat, 7am–7pm)

### Optimization
- Respond to LSA leads within 5 minutes — Google tracks response time and it affects ad ranking
- Mark leads as "booked" or "not a fit" consistently — LSA uses this data to optimize
- Request Google reviews from every completed job; review count directly affects LSA visibility
- Dispute leads that are clearly wrong category (people wanting a truck, not labor)

## 5. Facebook & Instagram Ads

### No mover verification required. Can launch immediately.

### Account setup
1. Create a Facebook Business Manager account at business.facebook.com
2. Add your Facebook Business Page
3. Install the Meta Pixel on your website (add to `<head>` of all pages — see tracking section)
4. Create a Custom Audience from website visitors (used for retargeting)
5. Verify your domain in Business Manager

### Campaign structure

**Campaign 1: Awareness (top of funnel)**
- Objective: Reach or Traffic
- Audience: Cold — geographic + demographic targeting
  - Locations: Salem, Keizer, Woodburn, Albany, McMinnville, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, Portland Metro (15-mile radius)
  - Age: 25–60
  - Interests: Moving and relocation, home improvement, apartment hunting, real estate
  - Life events: "Recently moved" (Meta life-event targeting option)
- Budget: 30% of Facebook budget
- Placements: Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Stories

**Campaign 2: Consideration (middle of funnel)**
- Objective: Lead Generation (using Meta lead form) or Traffic
- Audience: Engaged — people who have interacted with your page or watched 50%+ of any video
- Budget: 30% of Facebook budget
- Note: Lead forms often convert better than click-through on mobile; test both

**Campaign 3: Retargeting (bottom of funnel)**
- Objective: Conversions
- Audience: Website visitors from last 30 days who did NOT submit the quote form
- Budget: 40% of Facebook budget (highest ROI campaign)
- Ad angle: "Still planning your move?" — remind, reassure, convert
- Requires: Meta Pixel installed and collecting 100+ monthly visitors before this campaign is effective

### Sample Ad Concepts

**Ad 1 — Direct / Offer**
Format: Single image (manifest card graphic or crew working)
Headline: Free Moving Labor Quote — Salem, OR
Primary text: You bring the U-Haul or PODS. We bring the crew — loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly. Flat hourly rate, no hidden fees. Free quote, usually within one business day.
CTA Button: Get Quote

**Ad 2 — Education / Trust**
Format: Carousel (one card per service)
Headline: Moving labor — for every part of the job
Primary text: Loading. Unloading. Packing. Unpacking. Furniture assembly. You arrange the truck or container — we handle everything else. Serving Salem & Portland Metro.
CTA Button: Learn More

**Ad 3 — Retargeting**
Format: Single image
Headline: Still planning your [current month] move?
Primary text: You checked us out — here's a free, no-obligation quote whenever you're ready. Crew sized to your job, flat hourly rate, no surprises.
CTA Button: Get Quote

**Ad 4 — Seasonal (Summer)**
Format: Single image or short video
Headline: Summer dates are filling up
Primary text: May through August is our busiest season. If you have a summer move coming, now is the time to lock in your crew. Free quote — usually within one business day.
CTA Button: Book Now

## 6. Nextdoor

### Why Nextdoor works for this business
Moving labor recommendations are common on Nextdoor — people regularly ask their neighbors for trusted local help. A business with genuine reviews and a strong local presence gets organic recommendations here that no ad budget can replicate.

### Setup
1. Claim the Nextdoor Business Page at business.nextdoor.com
2. Complete the full profile with description, services, hours, phone
3. Use the same brand photos as GBP and Facebook for consistency
4. Ask satisfied customers to recommend the business on Nextdoor directly

### Paid Nextdoor options
- **Local Deal:** One-time offer posted to specific neighborhoods. Good for seasonal pushes.
- **Neighborhood Sponsorship:** Ad shown to residents in specific zip codes. Best for dense residential areas with high move rates.
- **Recommendation solicitation:** Cannot be paid for — must be earned. Prioritize this over any paid Nextdoor product.

### Before spending any money on ads, complete all of these:

**1. Meta Pixel installation**

Also add a Lead event to thank-you.html to track quote form completions:
```html
<script>fbq('track','Lead');</script>
```

**2. Google Analytics 4**
Add GA4 tracking code to every page (see README.md for the code snippet). Create a Conversion event for quote form submissions.

**3. UTM parameters**
Tag every paid link with UTM parameters so Google Analytics attributes traffic correctly:
```
?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=summer_2025
?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=salem_moving_labor
?utm_source=nextdoor&utm_medium=paid_local&utm_campaign=neighborhood_sponsorship
```

**4. Call tracking number**
Use a separate phone number for paid ads (e.g., through CallRail or Google's built-in call forwarding) so you can attribute phone leads to specific campaigns. This is critical for measuring LSA effectiveness, since many customers call rather than fill out the form.

**5. Lead source log**
Even a Google Sheet works. Columns: Date | Lead Source | Service Requested | Booked Y/N | Job Revenue. Review monthly. This single spreadsheet tells you more than any platform's self-reported dashboard.

### Monthly KPIs to track

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Cost per lead, by channel | Identifies which channels are efficient |
| Lead-to-booking rate, by channel | Identifies lead quality differences between channels |
| **Cost per booked job, by channel** | The number that actually matters — not just leads |
| Organic vs. paid lead mix | Sanity-checks whether paid is additive or cannibalizing organic |
| Average job revenue | Needed to calculate ROAS (return on ad spend) |
| Phone call rate (LSA) | Tracks response time and conversion rate on LSA leads |

## 8. Launch Sequence

Execute in this order to minimize wasted spend:

**Week 1–2: Foundation**
- [ ] Complete Google mover verification check (before any Google spend)
- [ ] Verify and fully complete Google Business Profile
- [ ] Install Meta Pixel and GA4 on website
- [ ] Set up UTM tracking system
- [ ] Create call tracking number for paid campaigns

**Week 3–4: First channel**
- [ ] Apply for Google Local Services Ads
- [ ] Complete LSA verification (background check, etc.)
- [ ] Set initial LSA weekly budget cap conservatively
- [ ] Create Facebook Business Page (if not done) and post first 5 posts organically

**Month 2: Expand**
- [ ] Review LSA cost-per-lead after 30 days
- [ ] If LSA is generating leads below $[TARGET CPL], increase budget
- [ ] Launch Facebook awareness campaign if website is getting 50+ visits/month
- [ ] Claim Nextdoor Business Page

**Month 3+: Optimize and scale**
- [ ] Launch Google Search Ads (if verification complete and LSA proven)
- [ ] Launch Facebook retargeting (requires 100+ monthly website visitors for audience size)
- [ ] Add Nextdoor paid placement in high-density residential neighborhoods
- [ ] Review all channel KPIs and reallocate budget to lowest cost-per-booking channel
