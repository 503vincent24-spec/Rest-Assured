# Social Media Campaign Strategy — Rest Assured Moving LLC
Version 2.0 | Complements FACEBOOK_CONTENT.md (20 evergreen posts)

---

## 1. Platform Priority

| Platform | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook** | Primary | Highest-intent local audience in 25–60 demographic; best for direct quote CTAs and service education |
| **Instagram** | Secondary | Visual content; Reels reach new audiences; repurpose Facebook content with format adjustments |
| **Google Business Profile posts** | Essential | Directly visible to high-intent searchers; free; often overlooked by competitors |
| **Nextdoor** | Supplemental | Hyper-local; excellent for neighborhood-level word of mouth |
| **TikTok** | Future | Lower priority until video content production is established; re-evaluate at 6 months |

---

## 2. Posting Frequency

| Platform | Frequency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 3–4x/week | Consistency matters more than volume |
| Instagram | 3x/week | 1–2 Reels + 1 static per week when video is available |
| GBP Posts | 1–2x/week | Use shorter versions of Facebook posts |
| Nextdoor | 1x/week or less | Nextdoor penalizes over-posting; quality over frequency |

---

## 3. Content Pillars

Rotate through these 6 content types across the week. Never post the same pillar two days in a row.

| Pillar | % of posts | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Service education** | 25% | How loading works, why packing technique matters, assembly tips |
| **Direct offer / CTA** | 20% | Free quote, book before summer, limited availability |
| **Trust & credibility** | 15% | Pricing transparency, licensing, before/after, manifest approach |
| **Tips & value** | 15% | Moving day prep, packing fragile items, measuring doorways |
| **Testimonials / social proof** | 15% | Star ratings, customer quotes, specific job outcomes |
| **Local / community** | 10% | Salem neighborhoods, service area highlights, local shoutouts |

---

## 4. Weekly Posting Template

Use this as a starting framework; adjust based on what performs.

| Day | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Direct offer / CTA | Single image or text graphic |
| Wednesday | Service education OR Tips | Carousel or short Reel |
| Friday | Testimonial OR Trust | Image with quote overlay |
| Saturday | Local / community | Photo or location-tagged post |

Add a 4th post on Tuesday or Thursday during peak season (May–August).

## 5. 12-Week Launch Campaign

For a new or refreshed page launching from zero.

**Weeks 1–2: Establish**
- Day 1: Pinned introduction post (Post 1 from FACEBOOK_CONTENT.md)
- Day 3: Service spotlight — Loading & Unloading
- Day 5: Pricing transparency post
- Day 8: Myth-busting FAQ ("Wait, you don't drive the truck?")
- Day 10: Tip post linking to Moving Day blog post
- Day 12: Behind-the-scenes / values post

**Weeks 3–4: Build Social Proof**
- Day 15: First testimonial (use a real one as soon as available)
- Day 17: Service spotlight — Furniture Assembly
- Day 19: Direct offer — "Free quotes, book your date"
- Day 22: Local / service area post
- Day 24: Packing fragile items tip
- Day 26: Second testimonial or review screenshot

**Weeks 5–6: Deepen Authority**
- Day 29: Full service list overview
- Day 31: "Why labor-only" educational post
- Day 33: Furniture assembly standalone post
- Day 36: Service area map / list post
- Day 38: Booking process walkthrough
- Day 40: Summer availability reminder (if seasonal)

**Weeks 7–12: Steady-State Rotation**
Rotate through the 6 content pillars using the weekly template. Introduce new content formats as you develop them (video, Reels, customer photos with permission).

## 6. Seasonal Campaigns

### Summer Moving Season — May through August
Peak season. Increase frequency to 4–5x/week. Add paid promotion to top-performing posts.

**Campaign theme:** "Book before your date fills up"
- Posts 1–2 weeks out from the season: "Summer moves are booking up"
- Mid-season: Real stats ("X jobs booked this week"), crew availability reminders
- Late season (August): "A few spots left — don't wait"
- Pair with boosted post spend on quote CTAs

**Creative direction:** High energy, urgency, social proof.

---

### New Year / January — Slow Season Opportunity
Counter-seasonal opportunity: lower ad costs, people making fresh-start decisions.

**Campaign theme:** "New year, new place — get it put together right"
- Furniture assembly push (people buy new furniture in January post-holidays)
- "Declutter before you move" content
- "Less competition for your moving date" angle
- Standalone assembly service spotlight

**Creative direction:** Clean, fresh, aspirational.

---

### Back-to-School / Apartment Turnover — August–September
Salem-area and Portland Metro apartment lease cycles create high August demand.

**Campaign theme:** "First apartment? We've got the heavy lifting."
- Smaller-job friendly messaging ("studio, 1BR welcome")
- Furniture assembly for new apartment setup
- "No job too small" positioning

**Creative direction:** Relatable, approachable, practical.

---

### Spring Refresh — March–April
Pre-season opportunity. Capture early planners before competitors ramp up.

**Campaign theme:** "Get ahead of summer move season"
- "Book now for May–June" urgency
- Home refresh + assembly (new furniture, reorganization)
- Blog post sharing (preparing for moving day)

**Creative direction:** Fresh, organized, ahead-of-the-curve.

## 7. Instagram & Reels Content Ideas

Short-form video (15–30 seconds) dramatically outperforms static posts in organic reach on Instagram. These ideas require only a phone and 2–3 minutes of filming on an actual job (with customer permission).

| Concept | Format | What to capture |
|---|---|---|
| Time-lapse load | Reel | Speed up a truck being loaded from empty to full |
| Assembly reveal | Reel | Finished furniture in the new room vs. pile of flat-pack pieces |
| Wrapping technique | Reel | Crew wrapping a dresser — shows skill and care |
| Before/after | Carousel | Empty rooms before loading vs. arranged rooms after unloading |
| Crew introduction | Reel | 15-second "meet the team" — builds trust |
| Moving day tip | Talking-head Reel | 30-second tip from a crew member |
| The manifest card | Static | Flat-lay of a job checklist — on-brand, visual, distinctive |
| Edge-stacked dishes | Reel | Correct vs. incorrect plate packing technique |

**Instagram Story Highlights to create:**
- "Services" — what we do, what we don't
- "Reviews" — testimonial screenshots
- "FAQ" — common questions answered
- "Book Now" — direct link to quote page

## 8. Local Partnership & Co-Marketing

These extend reach without ad spend and are uniquely suited to a labor-only business model.

### Truck rental companies
U-Haul, Penske, and Budget dealers in Salem get asked "do you know anyone who can help load this?" constantly. A referral arrangement — business cards at the counter, a mention on their location page, or a simple word-of-mouth relationship — costs nothing and produces highly qualified leads.

**Approach:** Walk in, introduce yourself, leave business cards, offer a small referral credit to their staff for bookings that mention the store.

### PODS / moving container providers
Same dynamic as truck rental. Container customers almost always need loading help and often don't know where to find it locally.

### Real estate agents in Salem
Agents speak to people about to move constantly. A "preferred vendor" card in their welcome packets, or a mention in their buyer/seller checklists, is natural placement. In exchange, refer them when customers ask about real estate.

**Approach:** Attend one Salem Association of Realtors event. Identify 5 agents to build relationships with. Offer reciprocal referrals.

### Salem-area apartment complexes and property managers
Move-in/move-out is a known pain point for residents. Offer to leave business card stacks in leasing offices, or offer a small discount for residents of a specific complex as a partnership.

### Storage facilities in Salem and Keizer
Customers moving into storage often need loading help. Natural overlap with the business model.

## 9. Content Calendar Tool Recommendation

A simple, free option: use a Google Sheets calendar with columns:
- **Date**
- **Platform** (Facebook / Instagram / GBP)
- **Content Pillar**
- **Copy** (paste the post text)
- **Visual needed** (Yes/No + description)
- **Status** (Draft / Scheduled / Published)
- **Performance** (Reach / Engagement — fill in after posting)

Review performance monthly. Identify top 3 performing posts by engagement rate. Create more content in the same vein. Retire formats that consistently underperform.

## 10. Measurement

Track monthly, even informally:

| Metric | Target (Month 3+) |
|---|---|
| Facebook page followers | Growing month-over-month |
| Average post engagement rate | 3–8% (good for local service) |
| Click-throughs to website | Track via UTM-tagged links in bio/posts |
| Quote requests citing "Facebook" | Track verbally at booking call |
| Google review count (from social ask) | +2 new reviews/month minimum |
| Nextdoor recommendations | Growing month-over-month |

**What not to obsess over:** Follower count in early months. Engagement rate and direct quote requests are better indicators of social media ROI than follower numbers.
