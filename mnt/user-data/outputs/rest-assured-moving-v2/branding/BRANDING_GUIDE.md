# Rest Assured Moving LLC — Brand Guide
Version 2.0 | Production

---

## 1. Brand Positioning

**What we are:** A moving labor specialist in Salem, Oregon and the Portland Metro area.
**What we are not:** A moving company that provides trucks, transportation, or hauling.

**The core insight:** People dread the *labor* of moving, not the paperwork of truck rental. We solve the dread.

**Positioning statement:** Rest Assured Moving LLC is the Willamette Valley's moving-labor specialist — the trained crew that loads, unloads, packs, unpacks, and assembles, so moving day is one less thing to worry about.

**Brand personality:** Capable · Calm · Exact · Warm
Think "the contractor who shows up on time and does it right" — not the slick national franchise, and not the neighbor with a pickup truck.

## 2. Logo System

### 2.1 Concept
A heraldic shield (trust, protection, a guarantee) containing a simplified house silhouette (the destination — home), with a bold checkmark replacing the doorway (job verified complete). No truck, van, dolly, or transportation iconography appears in any variant.

### 2.2 Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `assets/logo-mark.svg` | Icon-only mark. Favicons, social avatars, embossed signage, anything square or constrained. |
| `assets/logo-lockup.svg` | Horizontal lockup — mark + wordmark + tagline. Website header, email signature, letterhead, invoice headers. |
| `branding/business-card-front.svg` | Front of business card — mark + wordmark + tagline + service list. |
| `branding/business-card-back.svg` | Back of business card — navy background, contact details. |

### 2.3 Clear Space
Maintain clear space equal to the height of the shield's cap on all sides. No text, photography, or other graphics may enter the clear space. At minimum, the icon-only mark must be 24px tall; the lockup must be at least 30px tall for legibility.

### 2.4 Logo Don'ts
- **No recoloring.** Only use the approved palette.
- **No trucks.** Never add, combine, or overlay transportation imagery.
- **No stretching.** Scale proportionally only.
- **No placement on busy backgrounds** without a white or navy background lock.
- **No drop shadows or effects.**
- **No rearranging** of the wordmark / sub-line relationship.

## 4. Typography

### Type Stack

| Role | Typeface | Fallback | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headline** | Georgia | `Georgia, 'Iowan Old Style', 'Times New Roman', serif` | H1–H4, wordmark, pull quotes, testimonial text. Conveys stability and trustworthiness — a deliberate contrast to generic moving-company boldness. |
| **Body** | System UI | `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` | Paragraphs, navigation, buttons, forms, lists. Fast, readable, familiar. |
| **Labels / Utility** | Courier New | `'Courier New', Courier, ui-monospace, monospace` | Eyebrows, manifest/checklist labels, job numbers, stamps, form labels, fine print. The "ledger" texture that ties to the checkmark motif. |

### Type Scale (CSS custom properties)

```
--text-xs:   0.75rem  (12px)
--text-sm:   0.875rem (14px)
--text-base: 1rem     (16px)
--text-lg:   1.125rem (18px)
--text-xl:   1.25rem  (20px)
--text-2xl:  1.5rem   (24px)
--text-3xl:  1.875rem (30px)
--text-4xl:  2.25rem  (36px)
--text-5xl:  3rem     (48px)
--text-6xl:  3.75rem  (60px)
```

### Hierarchy Rule
- **Serif** for anything that should feel like a statement or promise (headlines, brand name)
- **Sans-serif** for anything that should feel efficient and scannable (body, forms, nav)
- **Monospace** for anything that should feel like a record or receipt (labels, job numbers, stats, stamps)

## 5. Voice & Tone

### The Voice Is
- Plain-spoken and specific — concrete details over vague claims
- Warm but not chatty — professional, not corporate
- Confident without arrogance
- Honest about scope (labor-only is always stated, not buried)

### Active Vocabulary
- **Use:** crew, movers, labor, loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, assembly, manifest, checklist
- **Avoid:** drivers, transport, hauling, delivery, freight, moving company (without "labor" qualifier)

### Copy Principles
1. Lead with what you DO before what you don't
2. Attribute the truck/container to the customer: "your truck," "the POD you've arranged"
3. Be specific — "$[X]/hour for a crew of 2" outperforms "affordable pricing"
4. Handle objections before they're raised (FAQ approach throughout)
5. End pages and sections with a clear, single CTA

## 7. Business Card Design

### Specifications
- Size: 3.5" × 2" (US Standard)
- Designed at: 1050 × 600px viewBox (300 DPI equivalent)
- Format: SVG (vector, losslessly scalable to any size)

### Front (`business-card-front.svg`)
- Background: Paper white (#FAFAF8)
- Top accent bar: Emerald 600, 10px
- Left third: Logo mark (scaled)
- Center/Right: Wordmark + "MOVING LABOR CO." sub-line
- Tagline: "Rest assured, it's handled." in italic
- Bottom: Dashed rule + service list in monospace

### Back (`business-card-back.svg`)
- Background: Navy 900 gradient
- Watermark: Oversized shield at low opacity
- Content: Company name, owner name/title, phone, email, website, service area list
- Accent: Emerald 500 for sub-headings and service area text

### Print Notes
- Export to PDF or high-res PNG before printing (SVG may not print from all printers)
- Use a professional printer (Moo, Vistaprint, or local Salem print shop)
- Request matte laminate for professional feel and durability
- Minimum print run: 250 cards

## 8. Signature Visual Element — The Manifest Card

A recurring "checklist/ledger card" appears across the website, print collateral, and social templates. Characteristics:
- Dashed rule dividers
- Checkbox-style checkmark bullets (emerald filled, white check)
- Monospace job numbers and status labels
- Slight rotation (0.6–0.8 degrees) to suggest a real paper document
- Appears in: website hero, social templates, quote summaries

This is the brand's ownable visual differentiator. Competitors use trucks, boxes, and generic "moving" imagery. We use a precision checklist. It visually earns the brand name — "Rest Assured" — by showing that nothing gets missed.

## 9. Labor-Only Brand Constraint

**This is non-negotiable and must be applied to every deliverable:**

- Never use the words "truck," "van," "vehicle," "transport," "haul," "drive," "delivery," or "freight" as services WE provide
- Always frame the truck/container as something the *customer* arranges
- Always append a labor-only disclaimer to formal documents (estimates, invoices, email templates)
- Never show or imply truck imagery in the logo, illustrations, or photography
- When in doubt: if the image shows wheels, don't use it
