## 3. About Section (Long-form "Our Story" tab)

Rest Assured Moving LLC is a Salem, Oregon-based moving labor company. We specialize in the parts of moving day that have nothing to do with driving a truck — and everything to do with doing the physical work correctly.

We handle loading and unloading for rental trucks, trailers, PODS, and moving containers. We pack and unpack boxes using the right materials and technique so nothing arrives broken. We assemble and disassemble furniture so it fits through doorways, survives the trip, and goes back together correctly on the other end.

**POST 11 — Tip: Moving Day Prep**
Pro tip: Clear your path before the crew arrives.

The single biggest factor in how fast a loading job goes is a clear, unobstructed path from your belongings to the truck door. Before we arrive:

✅ Move rugs out of hallways
✅ Relocate plants, coatstands, and decorative items
✅ Prop doors open if possible
✅ Reserve a parking spot for the truck near the entrance

These five minutes of prep can save you 30 minutes of job time. 💡
