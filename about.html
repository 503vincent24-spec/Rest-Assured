Our story

We built the company we kept wishing existed.

There's a gap in the moving industry that most people don't notice until they're standing next to a rented truck at 8am with nowhere to turn: plenty of truck rental options, not enough trustworthy hands to fill them.

Every moving company we saw either did everything — truck, crew, transport — or nothing. Nobody was focused exclusively on the labor side. The loading. The packing. The careful wrapping of furniture and the strategic filling of every cubic foot. The furniture reassembly that turns a new house into a functional home. The parts that are actually the hardest.

So we built that company. Rest Assured Moving LLC is a Salem, Oregon-based moving labor specialist. We don't own trucks. We don't haul freight between cities. What we do — and what we're built exclusively to do — is provide the trained, careful crew that handles everything around the truck you've already arranged.

Labor-only, by design

Our model exists because specialization produces better results. A crew that does nothing but loading, packing, and assembly gets exceptionally good at those things. You get specialists, not generalists.

Your truck, your control

You choose your rental truck, POD, or container. You control that cost. We show up with the crew, the equipment, and the technique to fill it correctly and protect everything inside.

Serving Salem since 2024

Locally owned and operated. We live and work in the communities we serve — Salem, Keizer, the Willamette Valley, and the Portland Metro area.

Our scope

What we do — and what we don't.

Being specific about our scope isn't a limitation — it's how we stay excellent at what we actually do. Understanding our model upfront means no surprises on moving day.

We are a moving labor company. We handle the physical work: loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly. We do not own trucks, drive vehicles, or provide transportation between locations. You arrange your own truck, trailer, PODS, or moving container — we provide the crew.

Most customers find this model works in their favor: they control the truck cost, keep the flexibility to book the container or rental that fits their timeline, and hire specialists for the part that actually requires skill and strength.

✓ We handle all of this:

  • Loading rental trucks, trailers, PODs, and containers
  • Unloading and room-by-room placement
  • Full or partial packing with proper materials
  • Unpacking and item placement
  • Furniture assembly and disassembly
  • Fragile item and specialty packing

✗ We do not provide:

  • ✗ Transportation or driving services
  • ✗ Truck or vehicle rental
  • ✗ Long-distance freight hauling
  • ✗ Storage units or warehousing
