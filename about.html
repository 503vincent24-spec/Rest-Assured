Our story

A crew built for the part of moving day people dread most.

Renting a truck or booking a container is the easy part. The hard part is the four hours of lifting, wrapping, and Tetris-ing a couch through a doorway — usually on the one day everyone's schedule is already stretched thin.

Rest Assured Moving LLC exists to take that part off your plate. We're a Salem, Oregon-based moving labor company built specifically around loading, unloading, packing, unpacking, and furniture assembly — not transportation. You decide how your belongings get from point A to point B; we make sure everything is packed right, loaded right, and put back together right on the other end.

What "labor-only" means for you

  • You choose your own truck, trailer, POD, or container
  • No fuel surcharges, mileage fees, or freight contracts
  • Book us for loading day, unloading day, or both
  • Crew sized to your home — studio to full house
  • Same trained team for packing, loading, and assembly
